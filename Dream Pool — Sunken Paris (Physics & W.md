A HyperRealistic 8K underwater short set in a crystal-clear, flooded Paris.
Two young women drift and laugh weightlessly through the sunken city of light, as golden sunlight filters between Haussmann façades and submerged monuments. Their movement obeys real fluid physics — buoyancy, drag, refraction, and light distortion.
The camera glides from a tranquil descent through boulevards to a radiant ascent above the Eiffel Tower, blending awe and serenity.

General Description
Environment

Submerged Paris, with turquoise gradients, light shafts between ornate façades, drifting petals from underwater trees, and caustic reflections over wrought-iron balconies. Coral and moss trace the marble of Pont Alexandre III, and schools of fish pass through the Eiffel Tower’s lattice.

Fluid: clear urban floodwater, low turbidity (0.2 mg/L suspended silica/silt)
Buoyancy: near-neutral, vertical drift ≈ 0.07 m/s

Sound: low-pass < 1.5 kHz, bubble crackle, deep bell-toned hum
