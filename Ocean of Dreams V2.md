# The Ocean of Dreams
## Subject / Scene Settings

- Audience: {locale:"JP"}; Narrative tone: ethereal, contemplative, infinite

- Subject type: environment

- Key features: endless cosmic sea reflecting nebulae and galaxies; gentle waves made of liquid starlight; giant koi fish of pure luminescence drifting beneath the surface, leaving shimmering trails; drifting constellations visible through translucent water; moonlit void above, where light falls like mist.

- Scale: horizonless expanse; koi span hundreds of meters, swimming in graceful arcs; depth perception dissolves into color gradients.

- Motion: slow, serene drift; mirrored ripples create parallax illusion; koi movements generate spiral light wakes; camera motion synchronized with wave oscillations.

- Environment: open celestial sea under a vast night void; faint planetary halos and cloudlike nebulae hover near the waterline.

- Lighting: primary from overhead moon (soft silver); secondary underlighting from glowing koi trails; tertiary from nebular reflections; volumetric rays drift through haze; bioluminescent motes orbit lazily above the surface.

- Grade: palette anchored in deep navy, indigo, magenta, and soft cyan with hints of warm coral; soft-contrast curve; luminous bloom on bright regions; smooth halation; minimal vignette; fine grain; crystalline reflections with light diffusion; no hard shadows.

- Visual taste: dreamlike cosmic realism; reflective HDR water; weightless physics; blend of real and surreal textures.

- Background / Location: infinite mirror-ocean under galactic sky dome.

## Camera

- Coverage: master push + 3 inserts (ripples, koi reflection, nebula refraction); consistent camera speed, 1° slow pan arc clockwise.

- Framing: starts WS (cosmic ocean + sky reflection), transitions to MS of koi crossing beneath, then back to WS as ripples dissipate.

- Motion: gimbal slow drift with slight sway to mimic tide rhythm; parallax through wave distortion; mild Deepth Of Field bloom sync’d with koi light.

- Lens/Focus: 28mm equivalent; smooth rack between reflection plane and koi beneath; shallow Deepth Of Field near surface ripples; creamy bokeh from bright starpoints.

- Occluders: floating particles, mist veils, refracted moon halo.

- Persist: same celestial palette, reflective plane, rhythmic pulse of koi light.
