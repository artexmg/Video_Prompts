Scene 1 (0 – 2 s) “Born of Lightning”

A vast black void. A violent, branching lightning bolt explodes across the sky. From its core, a small glowing cat (Bakeneko) forms from shimmering dots. The thunder crack echoes as its eyes ignite with light.
FX: Particle attractor pulls dots inward. Audio: loud thunder + cute meow (A).

Scene 2 (2 – 4 s) “The Walk Through Storm”

The Bakeneko pads forward gracefully; white dots rain upward and swirl like snow in reverse. Each step sends ripples through the dot field. Far ahead, faint shadows move.
Lighting: lightning flashes silhouettes; camera side-tracks. Audio: low rumble and soft wind rise.

Scene 3 (4 – 6 s) “The Reaper and the Heart"

From the darkness, a tattered Reaper appears — ripped robes, skeletal bones visible through holes, cradling a realistic, beating heart made of faint dots. The cat halts, tail flicking.
FX: robe edges tear into particles and reform. Audio: bone clicks + hollow breath.

Scene 4 (6 – 8 s) “Courage Pulse”

Close on the cat — its dots vibrate, forming a concentric halo. Lightning flashes behind as wind freezes. It lets out a low growl, meow pitch sliding down into a rumble.
FX: Dot density oscillates; wave ripple through scene. Audio: meow A → deepening transition.

Scene 5 (8 – 10 s) “The Transformation”

The Bakeneko erupts into a vortex of dots; its form stretches and splits into two streaming tails — the Nekomata emerges colossal. Lightning strikes synchronize with each growth pulse.
Camera: 360° orbit; particle turbulence and curl noise. Audio: massive Meow B with sub-bass and thunder.

Scene 6 (10 – 13 s) “The Clash”

The giant Nekomata lashes out with twin tails; the Reaper raises its scythe of bones. They collide in waves of particles; the Reaper disintegrates into a storm of dots and ash. The child is shielded by a glowing aura.
FX: particle collisions with interference ripples. Audio: impact boom + thunder roll + echo of deep meow.

Scene 7 (13 – 15 s) “Return and Release”

Silence. Dots float up as the Nekomata collapses into a spiral and shrinks back into the small cat. The Heart beats, glows and disperse. The Bakeneko sits calmly and lets out a gentle meow. Fade to black with title: “Bakeneko’s Redemption.”
Audio: soft meow (A) + distant bell; BGM fades to air.
