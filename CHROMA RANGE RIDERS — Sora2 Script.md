CHROMA RANGE RIDERS — Sora2 Script

Length: 15 s Aspect: 9:16 FPS: 30 Quality: High-Def, filmic

CORE CONSTRAINTS

Use the horses from the anchor; preserve anatomy and proportions.

No melting, warping, or cartoon limbs.

World is a Wild West prairie made of sculpted acrylic ribbons:

mesas, buttes, scrub, and fences all formed from layered paint.

Ground = semi-solid glossy paint dust / crusted acrylic.

Hooves always have weight and leave compressed tracks.

GLOBAL LOOK

Style: 3D quilled-paint / impasto sculpture (same as image).

Palette:

Horses: bright pastel magentas, cyans, yellows, whites.

Environment: desert ochres, burnt oranges, teal shadows, dusty violets.

Lighting: low late-afternoon “Western” sun, long shadows, warm key, cool rim.

Camera language: sweeping Western commercial — cranes, tracking shots, lens flares.

PHYSICS & FX

Dust = clouds of tiny pastel paint flecks.

Grass = short vertical paint ribbons flicking in the wind.

When hooves hit: thick dusty paint bursts + ring ripples in the crust.

Far mesas “unfold” like peeled paint strips in parallax.

Occasional lens streaks from sun hitting glossy paint ridges.

AUDIO

Music: modern cinematic Western

deep drums + handclaps, low cellos, a hint of twangy electric guitar.

FX:

Heavy hoof thuds in sync with drums.

Crunchy paint-dust impacts.

Wind with a soft “bristle-brush” texture.

Mix: quieter in wide vistas, punchier in close gallop shots.

SCENE BREAKDOWN
S0 — RANGE INTRO (0.0–0.5 s)

Lens: 24mm wide, high angle.

Start on a vast paint prairie: rolling acrylic dunes, ribbon cacti, distant mesas of stacked paint slabs.

In the far mid-ground, the horses are tiny silhouettes kicking pastel dust.

Audio:
Soft wind + distant hoof rumbles, first guitar note plucked.

S1 — SUN & HORIZON (0.5–1.5 s)

Lens: 35mm, slow push-in along the horizon line.

The sun is a coiled golden paint disc half-sunk behind mesas.

Heat haze is simulated as wobbly, transparent paint ripples.

Action:

The tiny silhouettes grow slightly as they approach.

FX:
Subtle flare across glossy ridges.

Audio:
Low strings and a rising pad, no big drums yet.

S2 — FIRST CLOSE GALLOP (1.5–3.0 s)

Lens: 35mm tracking, low ¾ front view of all the horses.

We’re now close: they thunder straight toward camera across a cracked paint plain.

Action:

Hooves pound, sending up powdery pastel dust that hangs like colored smoke.

Mane ribbons stream backward in the wind, matching the original palette.

FX:

Paint dust swirls in slow, heavy curls.

Ground compresses under each hoof, leaving sculpted hoofprints.

Audio:
Beat drops: deep drums in sync with hooves; gritty “paint dust” whooshes.

S3 — SIDE RANGE SHOT (3.0–4.5 s)

Lens: 50mm, side-on tracking shot.

Camera glides parallel to them, like a Western commercial.

Action:

Horses run in a perfect line, each with slightly different stride.

Background prairie streaks by: fences made of bent paint strips, ribbon shrubs.

FX:

Dust trails stretch backward like pastel comets.

A loose strand of paint-grass gets kicked up and spins beside them.

Audio:
Handclap layer added; rhythmic hoofbeats left-right in stereo.

S4 — CANYON OF COLOR (4.5–6.0 s)

Lens: 24mm, slight drone-like top view, then tilt down.

They burst between two towering paint mesas, forming a canyon.

Action:

As they pass, the inner canyon walls unroll in waves of color, revealing fresh stripes of turquoise, magenta, and gold.

FX:

Small paint chips flake off the cliffs and drift like confetti.

Light catches edges, creating glints and micro-flares.

Audio:
Music swells; subtle echo added to hoof hits (canyon reverb feel).

S5 — SPLIT & REJOIN (6.0–8.0 s)

Lens: 28mm, aerial three-quarter shot.

Action:

The herd splits around a single isolated paint butte:

Left horse veers left, right horse veers right, center rides straight.

As they pass, their hoof paths carve a glowing paint trails in the ground for each one.

After clearing the butte, they merge back into formation, trails converging like a tricolor Y-shaped brand.

FX:

Trails stay as raised, glowing ribbons behind them.

Audio:
Short melodic motif on guitar as they split and rejoin.

S6 — RIDGE CREST (8.0–10.0 s)

Lens: 35mm, front low angle climbing with them.

Action:

Horses charge up a paint ridge toward camera.

As they crest, the entire paint West unfolds behind them:

rivers of blue acrylic, distant spiral sun, layered mesas.

FX:

Wind pulls a few mane strands into long stretched ribbons that snap back.

Soft volumetric rays from the sun streak through dust.

Audio:
Music hits heroic peak; big drum + sustained chord.

S7 — SLOW-MO HERO SHOT (10.0–13.0 s)

Lens: 50mm, shallow DOF, slight orbit.

Action:

Slow motion: all the horses in the foreground, mid-gallop.

Dust and paint flecks float like stars around them.

Background softly blurred mesas and sky.

FX:

Individual flecks of paint glint as they pass the lens.

Micro ripples in their ribbon musculature — subtle, not liquid.

Audio:
Tempo feels half-time here; deep, emotional strings, echo of hoof hits.

S8 — BRAND OF THE RANGE (13.0–15.0 s)

Lens: 40mm, static but very slow push-out.

Action:

The horses gallop away from camera toward the infinite prairie.

Their glowing trails merge and spread into a wide, stylized paint brand on the ground.

As they recede, trails at the bottom of frame twist together and write the title in raised acrylic:

CHROMA RANGE RIDERS

Letters are made of three colors, slightly glowing.

FX:

Last specks of dust fall and freeze into tiny paint droplets.

Sun flares softly across the title.

Audio:
Final resolving chord, gentle guitar harmonic, then fade out at 15.0s.
