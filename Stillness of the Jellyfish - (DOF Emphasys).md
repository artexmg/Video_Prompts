“Stillness of the Jellyfish” (DOF Emphasis Edition)

15 seconds · hyper-real · shallow depth-of-field underwater cinematography

Format & Look

Duration 15s · 24fps · 180° shutter

Hyper-real 8K capture

Very shallow DOF in medium and macro shots

Bokeh rendered naturally in water (diffuse, pillowy blur, no harsh shapes)

Subtle halation on highlights

Color space: DCI-P3 with cool cyan/teal palette

Depth of Field Notes (Global)

Foreground jellyfish always tack-sharp.

Background jellyfish dissolve into soft blur at varying depths.

Caustics and particles behind subject bloom into soft glowing bokeh.

Lens pulls subtly adjust focus to emphasize the “foreground plane.”

Environment & Lighting

Depth 10–15 m below surface

Sunlight filtering down, creating shimmering caustics

Mild particulate drift

Background fades into teal haze

Camera & Scene Breakdown
Shot 1 — Foreground Float (0–5s)

Lens: 35 mm, shallow DOF
Framing: Medium close-up
Camera motion: Slow forward drift, slight parallax

One jellyfish in the foreground, perfectly sharp.

Background cluster (2–3 jellyfish) fully blurred, creating oceanic bokeh.

Soft sunlight shafts drift behind, exaggerated blur to emphasize depth.

Purpose: Establish DOF-driven aesthetic — crisp subject, dreamy soft-depth world behind.

Shot 2 — Drift Through Light (5–10s)

Lens: 50 mm, very shallow DOF
Framing: Tight portrait
Camera motion: Slow arc around the foreground jellyfish

The bell undulates gently, perfectly sharp; tentacles pass close to lens.

Background falls into creamy blur: caustic streaks and muted silhouettes.

Occasional micro-focus shifts (gentle breathing pulls, not abrupt).

Purpose: Create intimacy and dimensional separation.

Shot 3 — Macro Ballet (10–15s)

Lens: 100 mm macro, extremely shallow DOF
Framing: Extreme close-up of bell edge
Camera motion: Tiny upward rise (~10 cm)

The rim detail is razor-sharp while all background washes into teal haze.

Particles behind create soft, floating bokeh dots.

Light caustics blur into glowing abstract patterns.

Purpose: Pure visual serenity through depth compression.

Physics

Jellyfish pulse slowly (1 pulse every ~3 seconds)

Tentacles have high-drag secondary motion

Particles drift backward relative to camera movement

No turbulence; ultra-calm environment

Color & Grade

Highlights: cyan-white

Mids: turquoise, lavender hints

Shadows: deep teal, lifted blacks

Clean, soft underwater diffusion

No heavy contrast; gentle roll-off on highlights

Sound Design

Underwater hum and distant droning boom

Micro bubble rises

No music, no foley, pure ocean ambient

Mix quiet: soft edges, no sharp transients

Stylistic Cues

“Shallow DOF underwater portraiture”

“Foreground sharp, ethereal background blur”

“Blue Planet × macro nature film aesthetics”

Avoid magical glow or cartoon bioluminescence
