SCENE TIMELINE
(0.0–1.0s) Punch Through the Moon

The squadron reaches the paper moon — and it suddenly tears open like a giant perforated sticker.
They dive inside as strips of moon-paper peel backward.

(1.0–2.0s) Origami Hyperspace Tunnel

Inside is a swirling tunnel made of spiraling folded paper strips.
Camera rides behind the lead X-Wing as the tunnel crinkles and snaps like cardboard in a tornado.

(2.0–3.0s) Paper Monsters Appear

Out of the folds: feral origami cranes, each the size of a delivery truck, flapping angrily.
One snaps at an X-Wing and misses, accidentally ripping a chunk of tunnel paper.

(3.0–4.0s) Chase Through the Folded Rift

Camera whips around as the squadron performs evasive rolls.
Cranes pursue like chaotic, badly-folded dragons.

(4.0–5.0s) Papercraft Debris Storm

The tunnel collapses.
Loose scraps, stickers, and glitter blast everywhere — absolute arts-and-crafts Armageddon.

(5.0–6.0s) Emergency Paper-Clip Maneuver

One pilot fires a giant paperclip torpedo, which unfolds mid-air into a bent metal boomerang, clearing debris.

(6.0–7.0s) Giant Origami Boss Emerges

A colossal moon-sized origami fox unfolds itself from the tunnel layers.
Comically angry.
Eyes made of googly stickers.

(7.0–8.0s) Fox Attacks

It blows a huge puff of glittery confetti wind.
Camera shakes, visibility low.
X-Wings wobble violently like light cardstock.

(8.0–9.0s) Squadron Regroups

Camera pulls back — pilots regroup in formation despite the chaos.
One ship is missing half a wing but still somehow flying because… paper.

(9.0–10.0s) Fold-Reality Split

Reality cracks open with a loud tearing sound.
A new world unfolds: a massive papercraft solar system, spinning like a mobile in a kindergarten classroom.

(10.0–12.0s) Epic Dive Into the Paper Solar System

Squadron dives toward a giant folded planet made of layered red origami.
Paper planets wobble and squeak as they pass.

(12.0–14.6s) Final Standoff Shot
