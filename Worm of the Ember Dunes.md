# Worm of the Ember Dunes

Format: Vertical 9:16, 30fps, 15s
Look: Ultra-cinematic, photoreal 8K texture detail, molten dust explosions, glowing orange backlight, blue-green armored scales, violent kinetic motion.
Reference: image uploaded

## VIDEO SUMMARY

A colossal armored sandworm erupts from a volcanic desert in full rage, then violently slams back into the ground, burrows through the earth in a glowing subterranean tunnel, and finally leaves behind a quiet, golden desert—fading to black.

## SUBJECT / SCENE SETTINGS

Tone: epic, brutal, volcanic-mythic
Subject type: colossal sandworm
Creature features: blue-green metallic scales, layered plates, glowing ember-lit mouth, volcanic dust spraying from every movement
Environment: cracked red desert, molten dust clouds, scorching sunset light
Color palette: deep oranges, smoky blacks, metallic teal, ember reds
Lighting: cinematic sunset rim light + volcanic under-glow
Scale: worm is skyscraper-sized
Motion feel: heavy, violent, earth-shattering

## SHOT LIST (15 seconds total)

- Scene 1 — “The Rage” (0.0s–4.0s)

Shot: Worm already fully erupted, towering in frame, roaring.
Camera: Low-angle push-in, slight handheld vibration from shockwaves.
Action: Ember-coated dust whips outward; rock shrapnel arcs past the camera; worm shakes its head, jaw wide.
Lighting: Sunset gold from left; eruption-orange glow from below.
FX: Shockwave distortion, micro-embers, dust flicker.
Sound: Deep creature roar, rumbling sub-bass tremor, cracking earth.

- Scene 2 — “The Impact Dive” (4.0s–7.0s)

Shot: The worm’s massive body slams violently back into the earth.
Camera: Fast dolly backward as ground collapses, then snap tilt-down following the plunge.
Action: Huge explosion of dust, molten debris, curling fire-like plume; earth fractures in concentric rings.
FX: Dynamic flying rocks, directional dust blast, heat haze, intense turbulence.
Sound: Thunderous impact boom, rushing debris, granular rattling.

- Scene 3 — “The Descent” (7.0s–11.0s)

Shot: Cross-section / surface-following shot showing the worm tunneling beneath the crust.
Camera: Tracking shot along the cracking surface, synced to the worm’s subterranean path.
Action: The ground bulges and rips open as the worm moves; dust geysers fire upward; glowing cracks pulse like magma veins.
FX: Debris bursts, glow sparks, subterranean tremors.
Sound: Low-frequency rumble, grinding stone, distant muffled roars.

- Scene 4 — “Stillness Returns” (11.0s–14.0s)

Shot: Wide, peaceful desert at dusk.
Camera: Slow upward tilt showing the empty landscape and glowing horizon.
Action: Dust settles; only soft ember motes drift in the air.
Lighting: Warm, calm sunset; faint smoke trails dissipating.
Sound: Wind slowly replacing the rumble; faint residual heat crackle fading.

- Final Moment — Fade to Black (14.0s–15.0s)

Visual: Slow dissolve to pure black.
Sound: A low bass rattle decays into silence.
