# Redline Requiem — F1 Spirit (15s, 8K UHD, Japanimation Realism)
## Subject / Scene Settings
- Genre: high-definition japanimation; hyperreal aesthetic with cinematic motion and lighting
- Tone: intense, high-adrenaline, heroic
- Theme: the essence of F1 racing — speed, precision, and obsession
- Audience: global; emphasis on Japanese anime realism (Akira / Initial D meets real F1)
- Subject type: F1 driver vs rivals; focus on the pilot’s determination, machine harmony, and speed physics
- Motion: continuous fast cuts between six POVs (trackside, cockpit, aerial, wheel cam, drone chase, pit edge)
- Lighting: harsh daylight → glints from metal and asphalt heat distortion → cool tunnel reflections
- Color/Grade: saturated reds and silvers; asphalt shimmer; depth-of-field with bokeh streaks; lens flares
- FX: motion blur, tire friction, turbo pops, vibration, dynamic heat haze, flying rubber bits, camera shake at G-forces
- Base Layer: continuous engine bed (pitched modulation by scene intensity)
- Sound: roaring engines, tire screech, echoing Doppler, crowd murmur fading under the roar, shifting gear clacks, Doppler sweeps
- Lens: variable focal lengths — 14mm ultra-wide for sweeps, 50mm shallow DOF for pilot POV; cinematic rack-focus transitions
- Music: hybrid orchestral + synth percussion underscoring mechanical tempo (140 BPM)

## Scene Sequence
1. **Opening — Trackside Omen (WS)**  
   A scorching racetrack mirage. Heat ripples distort two F1 cars as they roar past. Dust kicked up. Engine roar fills the air.
2. **Cockpit POV — The Pilot’s Pulse (CU)**  
   Inside helmet HUD view: tachometer redlining; gloved hands shifting gears; heartbeat syncs with revs.
3. **Aerial Sweep — Twin Comets (WS)**  
   Drone shot following both cars through an S-curve, shimmering reflections and jet-trail heat distortion.
4. **Wheel Cam — The Friction Line (ECU)**  
   Tire cam close-up: spinning slicks grind asphalt; sparks flicker under the diffuser; rubber particles flying.
5. **Overtake — The Inferno Burst (MS)**  
   Dynamic side-mounted chase; the rival’s car slips ahead; pilot downshifts, exhaust flares violently, camera whips past.
6. **Final Lap — Victory Surge (CU→WS)**  
   Pilot’s eyes in reflection of visor; camera pulls back to massive track-wide shot as the car crosses the finish under roaring heatwaves.

## Audio Design
- Engine layers (V12 roar + hybrid whine + low-end subrumble)
- Pavement friction fx with Doppler transition
- Reverb tails in tunnel segments
- Clattering pit radio fragment (“Push, push, last lap!”)
- Subtle orchestral hit synced to final burst

[2 s – 4 s] → Heartbeat synth
[4 s – 7 s] → Build percussion

[10 s – 15 s] → Triumphant sustain + ambient fade
