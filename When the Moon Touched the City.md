# Sora2 Prompt — “When the Moon Touched the City”

Format:
8K HDR10 · 24 fps · 2.39:1 aspect ratio
Color Space: DCI-P3 cinematic tone mapping
Lens: 35 mm anamorphic · f/1.8 · shallow DOF · chromatic bokeh
Lighting: high-contrast moonlight diffusion with volumetric haze
Motion Style: temporal acceleration with hyper-real motion blur

Prompt Summary

A futuristic city sleeps beneath an immense full moon. A lone woman stands on a skyscraper rooftop as time fractures, accelerates, and bends around her. Days and centuries flicker past in seconds. Gravity distorts. The moon descends, merging its reflection with her — until the world rebirths in silence and dawn.

Scene 1 — The Stillness Before (0 – 2 s)

Visuals:
Slow dolly toward skyscrapers blanketed in fog; distant city lights glow faintly through haze.
The moon dominates the sky, perfectly still.
Physics:
Volumetric fog dynamics, micro-particle drift, gentle air currents bending light beams.
FX:
Lens flares from window reflections, slow-moving dust motes under blue light.
Audio:
Low-frequency hum, faint wind whisper, heartbeat-paced pulse at 40 Hz.

Scene 2 — Time Fractures (2 – 5 s)

Visuals:
A pulse of light radiates from the moon — suddenly, time accelerates.
Skylines flicker between night and day; car trails become flowing ribbons.
The woman remains frozen while clouds swirl violently.
Physics:
Temporal compression (motion-vector blending), Doppler light shift, multi-layer volumetric simulation.
FX:
Hyper-speed sky timelapse, bloom trails, shadow streaks, multi-exposure motion composite.
Audio:
Crescendo of reversed wind + ticking clocks morphing into digital static; sub-bass rumbles synchronizing with the light pulse.

Scene 3 — Lunar Descent (5 – 8 s)

Visuals:
Time halts again. The moon lowers closer, filling half the sky.
Its reflection moves across glass towers like liquid silver.
Fog spirals upward; light bends around her silhouette.
Physics:
Gravitational lensing distortion, soft refraction warping edges, particle suspension in partial zero-G.
FX:
Refractive light arcs, caustic patterns across fog, faint aurora halo forming.
Audio:
Low-frequency atmospheric resonance, metallic creak of the city bending, subtle heart-beat echo.

Scene 4 — Hyper-Speed Resonance (8 – 12 s)

Visuals:
The world erupts into hyper-speed.
Storms, sunsets, seasons — all flicker within seconds.
Skyscrapers decay and rebuild; the sky becomes a time-lapse storm of light.
The woman’s hair and dress ripple in invisible currents.
Physics:
Dynamic time-warp simulation, environmental morphing with high-frequency temporal blur, air-vortex generation around her.
FX:
Light trails, window flicker loops, thunder flashes illuminating entire cityscape, streaked neon reflections across fog.
Audio:
Cyclonic whoosh + crackling thunder layered with digital chime motifs; binaural time-shift sweeps panning left-right; sub-harmonic swell matching lunar pulse.

Scene 5 — The Merge (12 – 14 s)

Visuals:
Her reflection inside the moon mimics her movements faster than reality — vibrating like light.
She reaches toward it; fingertips align in mid-air.
The entire city folds into streaks of light, drawn upward toward the moon.
Physics:
Quantum-level light fragmentation, voxel dispersion, gravity inversion, volumetric implosion with soft-edge collapse.
FX:
Particle beam convergence, prism refraction bursts, shockwave ripple across fog and glass, full-spectrum light flare.
Audio:
Ethereal choral pad building with reverse-reverb swells; bass drop into silence at fingertip contact.

Scene 6 — Rebirth (14 – 15 s)

Visuals:
Silence.
The fog settles.
The moon shrinks, soft and distant again.
She stands alone — serene — as dawn light cuts through the mist.
A single tear floats upward instead of falling.
Physics:
Post-shockwave drift, gentle atmospheric particles returning to normal gravity.
FX:
Light diffusion glow, chromatic fade, dust glinting in sunrise.
Audio:
Soft piano note, faint exhale, city hum returns — reality restored.

Tone

Emotionally transcendent — a visual symphony of time and light, combining dreamlike calm with hyper-real cosmic physics.
