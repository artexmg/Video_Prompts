TIDAL VEIL (15 sec · 8K UHD · 30fps)

Reference: Rio de Janeiro coastal city — Atlantic beachfront, dense high-rise shoreline, steep tropical granite mountains, Christ the Redeemer atop Corcovado.

STYLE & VISUAL LANGUAGE

Hyper-detailed anime realism with sculptural miniature-city precision
Crisp ink-like edge definition on water, architecture, and foliage
Extreme scale contrast: ornamental city vs. god-scale ocean
Teal / turquoise Atlantic water contrasted with warm gold, coral, and emerald land tones
Volumetric spray, filamented foam strands, refractive highlights
High-contrast cinematic grading — glowing highlights, deep shadow wells
Tropical Atlantic lighting: humid air diffusion, warm coastal glare
No passive shots — every frame driven by force, gravity, and momentum

AUDIO

Low-frequency sub-bass swell building beneath the scene
Wind howling through dense beachfront towers and mountain vegetation
Creaking boats, snapping cables, rattling metal structures
No music until final seconds — pure environmental tension
Wave impact roar syncs precisely with visual collapse

CAMERA LANGUAGE

Aggressive, energized motion only — zero static frames after anchor
Hybrid scale camera: miniature macro passes → colossal wide sweeps
Fast parallax exaggerates vertical city rise and wave height
Lens progression: 35mm → 18mm → 50mm → 12mm → 70mm macro → 24mm wide
Occasional lens contamination: spray, droplets, refraction distortion

ENVIRONMENT & PHYSICS

Water behaves with immense mass and crushing weight
Foam fractures into hairline filaments and tangled tendrils
Vortices curl inside the wave wall; subsurface pressure visible
Boats strain against moorings, hulls pitching violently
Vegetation ripples in shockwaves from wind pressure
Clouds churn and shear as if pulled by the wave’s gravity

GOAL & TONE

Capture the moment just before annihilation —
overwhelming scale, terrifying beauty, divine indifference.
Rio feels fragile, ornamental, and doomed beneath unstoppable nature.
