THE TERRITORY

Duration: 25 seconds
Aspect Ratio: 16:9 cinematic
Style: Hyper-real cinematic wildlife tension
Tone: Primal dominance / quiet threat / human intrusion

RULE ZERO

❌ No stylization
❌ No slow motion
❌ No exaggerated VFX
❌ No dramatic attack or gore

Everything feels real, observational, and tense.

The lions are not chasing.
They are escorting.

SHOT LENGTH LAW

Average: 0.7–1.3 s
Max: 1.6 s

Cuts triggered by:
tire impacts
dust bursts
lion movement
engine shifts
Camera always in motion.
AUDIO

BGM:
Low ominous cinematic score
Deep drums + distant tribal percussion
Sparse bass pulses
Slow rising tension

SFX:
off-road engine roar
gravel impacts
wind across dry grass
lion breathing / distant growls
Music stays restrained and tense, never heroic.

SEQUENCE
0.0 – 1.5 s
Bronco already moving fast on a dirt track.
Dust exploding behind tires.
Savanna stretching endlessly.

1.5 – 3.0 s
Low side tracking shot.
Two lions appear in the grass running parallel to the vehicle.
They are calm.
Effortless.

3.0 – 4.5 s
Front-quarter shot of Bronco.
Driver notices movement.
Another lion pacing on the opposite side of the road.
Dust swirling between them.

4.5 – 6.0 s
Ground-level camera.
Lion paws striking dry earth at high speed.
Dust kicking with each stride.
Vehicle slightly behind the animal.

6.0 – 8.0 s
Wide shot.
Three lions now visible around the vehicle.
Not chasing.
Escorting.
The car looks small.

8.0 – 10.0 s
Camera inside the Bronco.
Through windshield: lions pacing on both sides.
Dust clouds moving like a storm.

10.0 – 12.0 s
One lion suddenly accelerates.
Runs ahead of the Bronco.
Crosses the road.

12.0 – 14.0 s
Lion stops in the middle of the track.
Vehicle approaching fast.
Dust billowing.

14.0 – 16.0 s
Driver slams the brakes.
Bronco skids.
Dust wave overtakes the vehicle.

16.0 – 18.0 s
Silence for a moment.
Lion standing in road.
Other lions slowly emerging from grass.

18.0 – 21.0 s
Wide overhead shot.
Bronco stopped.
Four lions surrounding the vehicle at a distance.
Completely calm.

21.0 – 24.0 s
Lion walks toward the camera.
Golden eyes locked forward.
Wind moving through mane.
Absolute authority.

24.0 – 25.0 s
Extreme close-up.
Lion staring directly into camera.
Dust settling.

Cut to black.
