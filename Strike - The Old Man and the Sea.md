🎭 Character Profile — Santiago (The Old Man)

A lean, sun-browned fisherman in his late 60s. Face etched with salt lines, gray stubble, clear blue-gray eyes. Thin silver hair under a worn straw hat. He wears a faded cotton shirt with rolled sleeves, frayed beige trousers, bare feet gripping the deck. Forearms veined and scarred from rope burns.
Material Physics: Light cloth responds to breeze; humidity adds weight; salt crystals sparkle on forearms and hat brim.

⛵ Boat Description

Small 16-foot wooden skiff painted faded turquoise. Weathered planks, rope coils, oarlocks, a patch-tied sail, and simple fishing gear.
Physics: Subtle deck flex and creaks with wave motion; wet sheen reflects morning light; salt stains visible on edges.

🌅 Environment & Lighting

Sea: Turquoise to deep cerulean gradient; gentle 20–40 cm swells. Light fog at horizon.
Sky: Pre-dawn pastel blue turning to warm orange-pink; cirrus strands stretching east.
Lighting: Low-angle golden hour sun casts long specular highlights on waves and hull; soft backlight for Santiago’s silhouette; volumetric mist particles illuminated at grazing angles.

🔊 Sound & Music Design

Ambient: Gentle lapping water, rope creaks, distant gulls, breeze through oarlocks.

Strike Cue: Silence → sudden thump and splash → string surge + brass accent.

Underwater: Low-pass filter ocean hum + bubble crackles + low cello drone (“heartbeat of the sea”).

🎞 Scene-by-Scene Cinematic Sequence
Scene 1 — Dawn Silence (0 – 3 s)

Camera: Wide aerial shot descending toward a lone skiff adrift in mirror-calm water. Slow dolly-in to Santiago from stern.

Lighting: First orange-pink sun rays break through haze, casting godrays and soft lens flare.

Action: Santiago sits motionless, hands resting on the line, eyes fixed ahead.

FX & Sound: Sea mist particles drift past lens; wood creaks; sparse cello note builds tension.

Scene 2 — The Line Trembles (3 – 7 s)

Camera: Push-in to close-up of Santiago’s hands and rope. Focus pull from knuckles to water reflection.

Action: A tiny jerk ripples through the line; Santiago inhales sharply.

Physics: Rope oscillation with micro wave response; hand tendons tense; skin compresses realistically.

Sound: Fibers tighten (creak), heartbeat pulse under soundtrack, thin violin harmonic for anticipation.

Scene 3 — The Strike (7 – 11 s)

Camera: Whip-pan to bow; hand-held-style close-up on rod bending. Snap zoom to water surface as it erupts.

Action: Marlin hits; boat rocks; Santiago braces, rope cuts into palms; spray bursts everywhere.

FX & Physics: Photoreal fluid simulation with splash particles refracting sunlight; lens droplets; rope recoil.

Lighting: Transition from warm dawn tones to brilliant sun glint as marlin flashes under surface.

Sound: Sudden silence → impact splash → orchestral brass accent → distant bass whale-tone.

Scene 4 — Below the Surface (11 – 15 s)

Camera: Cross-dissolve into underwater tracking shot following the line downward through golden shafts of light.

Action: The marlin appears as a massive shadow gliding in slow motion; its scales glimmer like liquid silver. Santiago’s silhouette distorts above.

Physics: Volumetric light scattering, bubble simulation, rope tension visible through refracted water.

Lighting: Cyan-gold gradient with caustics flickering over marlin’s body; depth fades into dark blue abyss.

Sound & Music: Low-pass ocean ambience with muffled pulse; cellos and contrabass swell into a single resonant note of awe.

🌤 Closing Direction

Camera slowly pulls back to reveal the enormous marlin turning under the tiny boat above, line stretched tight between man and nature. Fade to blinding sunlight through water — a metaphor for the moment destiny strikes.
