STRATA OF HUNGER

Duration: 15 seconds
Resolution: 8K
Frame rate: 24 fps
Aspect: vertical (9:16) or cinematic (16:9) — both work
Style: hyper-detailed surreal realism · organic strata · sculptural biology

VISUAL STYLE & WORLD RULES

The reef is not coral — it is layered geological flesh, like compressed sediment, paper strata, or carved bone
Every surface shows thin stacked ribbons, slightly uneven, organic, tactile

Color palette:
Warm mineral layers (ochre, coral red, pale yellow)
Cold predator tones (bone white, slate blue, steel gray)
Water is clear but heavy, particles always drifting
No fast cuts — motion feels ancient, inevitable

SCENE TIMELINE (15s)
0.0 – 2.5s | The Strata
Extreme macro drift across reef layers
Camera glides parallel to the strata, emphasizing depth and age
Tiny particles fall like underwater dust
SFX:
Low, distant sub-bass ocean rumble
Soft particulate hiss (like sand sliding underwater)

2.5 – 5.5s | The Shape Beneath
Reef layers subtly separate and flex
A shadow moves inside the strata, not yet visible
Small fish hesitate mid-swim
SFX:
Deep stone-like creaking, slow and heavy
Water pressure groan (very low frequency)

5.5 – 8.5s | Emergence
The shark’s snout pushes out of the reef, as if breaking through rock
Its body texture perfectly matches the layered reef — same material, same logic
No aggression yet — just inevitability
SFX (very audible):
Layered stone separation sound (rock + damp paper + underwater crack)
Water displacement surge
Fish scatter with sharp fin flicks

8.5 – 11.5s | Predator Revealed
The shark slides fully free
Light shifts colder, bluer
Its jaw opens slightly — not a snap, just weight and threat
Camera subtly tilts upward to give dominance
SFX:
Slow jaw tension creak
Heavy exhale bubbles from the shark’s gills
Ocean rumble deepens

11.5 – 14.0s | Stillness After Birth
Shark holds position, suspended
Reef behind it settles back into silence
Turtle continues walking calmly in foreground — life continues
SFX:
Ambient water flow
Distant whale-like harmonic (very subtle, non-musical)

14.0 – 15.0s | End Card (Silent Text)
Minimal text fades in, etched as if carved from the strata texture:
“Hunger is ancient.”
SFX:
One final low sub-bass swell, then silence
AUDIO DESIGN RULES (IMPORTANT)
No music
No narration
All sound must feel physical
Emphasize:
Pressure
Weight
Material friction
Sounds should feel felt, not just heard

CAMERA & MOTION NOTES
Slow dolly and lateral slides only
No handheld
No fast zooms
Everything moves like it’s been moving for millions of years
