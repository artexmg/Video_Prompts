THE FRIENDLY NEIGHBORHOOD KRAKEN ( 8K HDR10 60fps · Cute Cinematic · Soft Pastel + Coastal Village)

CHARACTER: The Kraken, who is artexmg.profoctavio

A giant, gentle Kraken, tries to help a tiny seaside village with everyday chores… and accidentally causes adorable chaos.

STYLE & LOOK
Pixar-cute proportions, soft round shapes, squishy tentacles
Pastel coastal palette: mint blue water, coral pink rooftops, sun-washed yellows
Kraken’s doodle-texture becomes glowing friendly glyphs that activate when it’s trying hard
Lighting: warm morning sun, soft volumetric haze, watercolor-bright reflections
Overall mood: wholesome, cozy, slightly chaotic but heartwarming

🎥 CAMERA / LENS / HDR
Resolution: 8K (7680×4320)
Frame Rate: 60fps
Color: DCI-P3 HDR10 (10-bit)

Lens Kit:
24mm wide f/2.8 for big village shots
35mm anamorphic f/2.0 for comedic medium shots
50mm f/1.8 for emotional close-ups
Gentle handheld-like float for cuteness
Medium depth of field—soft, creamy bokeh on foreground charm elements

🪄 FX & PHYSICS
Water & Splash Sim
Micro-splashes when tentacles move
Soft underwater bloom on wet surfaces
Water droplets drifting off tentacles in slow arcs
Environmental FX
Floating dust motes in sunbeams
Light fog from morning ocean breeze
Cute Chaos FX
Boats wobble but never flip
Tiny fish hop out and shake their heads in comedic timing
Floating heart-shaped bubbles whenever the Kraken smiles
Texture Magic

Doodle-glyphs on its skin pulse/brighten when it’s “trying to be helpful”

🔊 AUDIO MIX

Ambience: soft seaside breeze, distant gulls, gentle dock creaks

SFX:

Splashy tentacle movements
Tiny boat wood squeaks
Mini villagers’ surprised squeaks
Heart bubble pops
Music:
Light-hearted marimba + ukulele plucks
Warm rising motif during emotional beats
End Card: soft bell + bubble FX

🎬 15-SECOND SCENE BREAKDOWN
📍 Scene 0 — Anchor (0.0–0.2s)

Close-up of the Kraken blinking sweetly at camera, holding a tiny broom in one tentacle.
Tiny heart bubble pops.

🏘️ Scene 1 — Helping the Village (0.2–3.0s)

24mm wide shot of a pastel coastal pier.
The Kraken uses a tentacle to gently place a fishing boat into the water—too gently, so the boat bounces and villagers wobble comically.
Kraken smiles nervously.

🧹 Scene 2 — “Sweeping” (3.0–6.0s)

Tracking shot as the Kraken attempts to sweep the pier with a giant broom.
Instead, the sweeping creates a huge gust that sends hats flying and papers spiraling.
Villagers laugh instead of getting upset.
Glowing glyphs pulse on Kraken’s skin as it tries harder.

👋 Scene 3 — The Wave (6.0–9.0s)

Medium shot, 35mm anamorphic.
The Kraken waves enthusiastically at kids on the shore.
A big splash sprays everyone—kids cheer.
Heart-shaped bubbles rise.

💛 Scene 4 — Sweet Moment (9.0–12.5s)

50mm close-up as a little girl pats one of its tentacles.
The glyphs glow bright yellow with joy.
Kraken lowers its head, touched.

✨ Scene 5 — End Card (12.5–15.0s)

Warm pastel village background.
Text fades in:

“Big heart.
Bigger splash radius.”

Soft bell + bubble whoosh.
