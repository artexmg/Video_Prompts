STRAND BIOLOGY

Duration: 15 seconds
Resolution: 8K
Frame Rate: 24 fps
Aspect Ratio: 16:9 (cinematic) or 9:16 (vertical)
Style: hyper-real underwater macro realism · material-first biology
No narration. No music. Natural sound only.

CORE CONCEPT

A normal reef ecosystem.
Animals behave exactly like real animals.
The only difference: their bodies are composed of millions of ultra-fine biological strands, densely packed like muscle fibers magnified.

This is not fabric.
This is not hair.
This is biological structure.

MATERIAL & BIOLOGY RULES

Bodies are made of parallel micro-strands, tightly bundled
Strands slide, compress, and shear subtly during motion
No loose threads, no fraying
Texture density increases near joints and muscles
Surfaces remain smooth at a distance, fibrous only up close
