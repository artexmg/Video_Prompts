SORA PROMPT (15s CUT)
Scene & Setting

A vast Roman Colosseum at the end of time. Every arch, stair, and balcony is packed with millions of figures, compressed into a living, woven mass. The stone appears eroded and organic, as if history itself has softened it. The arena floor remains unseen — what matters is the gathering.

Global Motion Rule:
No static frames. Crowd micro-shifts continuously. Dust drifts at all times.

TIMELINE

0–2s — Arrival
High, wide overhead shot of the Colosseum. Warm, dusty sunlight cuts through the air. A low, distant murmur fills the space. The arena is quiet.

2–5s — Density
Slow lateral glide across arches. The sheer scale becomes overwhelming — layers of red, gold, and ash merge into an almost textile surface. Dust motes float in slow motion.

5–8s — Reaction
The crowd subtly leans forward together, responding to something unseen. Fabrics ripple. A single banner detaches and falls, trailing through the air.

8–11s — Stillness
Macro pass through silhouettes of spectators. Faces dissolve into texture and motion. A faint tremor ripples through the stone, like an exhale.

11–13s — Quiet Shift
Crowd sound fades. Wind replaces it. Light warms and softens.

13–15s — End Card
Cut to black. Minimal white text appears:

“WHAT HAPPEN NEXT"
Mix your reality”
