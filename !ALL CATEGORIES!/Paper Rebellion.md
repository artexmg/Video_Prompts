“PAPER REBELLION I: DAWN STRIKE”

(15 seconds — ultra-dynamic, zero dead moments)

STYLE & AESTHETIC BLOCK

premium cut-paper diorama look
layered sheets of textured cardstock (visible fibers, tactile shadows)
smooth cinematic lighting with orange dawn glow
all objects behave like light paper, edges flutter in airflow
ocean made of stacked, waving blue paper strips
skyline is rigid but with slight wobble from turbulence
spaceships have clean origami folds and subtle bending under force
soft volumetric paper-dust particles on fast turns

GLOBAL MOTION RULES

NO STATIC MOMENTS, NO FREEZE FRAMES
camera always drifting, gliding, or tracking
paper layers ripple with atmospheric pressure
shadows animate subtly
all spaceships show micro-vibrations from flight
DO NOT treat the city as a still background — add micro-wobble & turbulence flutter

SCENE TIMELINE (immediate action, short cuts)
(0.0–1.0s) ACTION FROM FRAME ONE — Squadron Flyby at Speed

Camera is already mid-air, rushing left-to-right as three spaceships blast past frame, paper edges fluttering violently.
Paper sky ripples like sheets shaken by wind.

(1.0–2.0s) Wide Formation Over City

Camera pulls out to reveal full squadron in formation over the skyline.
Buildings wobble slightly from the passing shockwave.

(2.0–3.0s) Low Pass Over Buildings

Low-angle shot — an spaceships streaks close above the tallest spires.
Paper skyscrapers bend just a little under the turbulence.
Cut-paper reflections shimmer on their faces.

(3.0–4.0s) Side-Tracking Shot With One spaceships

Camera locks to one spaceships from the side as it speeds forward.
Wings vibrate; paper dust lifts behind it.
Ocean layers below ripple violently.

(4.0–5.0s) Paper Seagull Near-Miss (fun moment)

An spaceships narrowly dodges it.
Feathers (tiny paper clippings) scatter.

(5.0–6.0s) Vertical Paper-Sky Punch

One spaceships pulls up sharply.

(6.0–7.0s) Water-Level Skim

Camera drops to ocean height.

(7.0–8.0s) Three-Ship Roll

Three spaceships roll in synced choreography.
Camera rolls with them (smooth, elegant), leaving trails of glowing paper dust.

(8.0–9.0s) Cockpit Close-Up

(9.0–10.0s) City Canyon Run

spaceships banks hard left; paper debris falls from a building edge.

spaceships fly over the sea; reflections distort as layered waves shift rhythmically.

(11.0–12.0s) Hero Formation Backlit by Dawn

Classic hero shot.

(12.0–14.6s) Ascend Toward the Moon

They rise toward a circular cut-paper moon, leaving rippling trails.
