# Dreamfall — The Sleepers Above: Shared Dreamspace Matrix

(8K UHD, 15s — Surreal Hyperrealism / Dream Physics / 24 fps)

---

## Concept Overview

**Layout:** 2×2 matrix of simultaneous dream panels — four different yet interwoven dream fragments happening at the same time, forming a unified shared dreamspace.

**Genre:** Surreal realism / dream physics
**Style:** Hyper-realistic 8K, cinematic slow motion, ethereal volumetric lighting
**Mood:** Tranquil, sacred, intimate — suspended between sleep and the deep sea
**Arc:** Curiosity → serenity → awe → mystery → transcendence (felt through contrast between panels, not sequence)

**Setting:** Each panel reveals a separate but linked dimension of the same dawn-lit room. Dust motes move as if submerged; curtains breathe; the sleepers’ pale dresses ripple and connect across panels through synchronized air currents.

---

## Physics Realism Notes

* **Fluid Behavior:** Air modeled with underwater drag; Re ≈ 100–300; diffusion density ≈ 1.003 kg/m³
* **Cloth:** Chiffon / light cotton; Cd ≈ 1.3–1.5; lag ≈ 0.25 s
* **Buoyancy:** Near-neutral; drift velocity ≈ 0.05–0.07 m/s
* **Hair Motion:** Low-frequency oscillation; curls separate and regroup; eddy decay ≈ 2 s
* **Optics:** IOR 1.02; refracted caustics drift with 10–15 cm cells at 0.25 m/s
* **Lighting:** Kelvin gradient 5000K → 8200K; soft bloom + haze; volumetric light scattering
* **Camera:** 35 mm eq., f/2.8, 1/200 s, ISO 400, WB 7500K
* **Audio:** Sub-1 kHz low-pass hum; heartbeat 60 bpm fading to silence

## Global Motion & Physics Integration

* All panels occur simultaneously and influence one another — each one emits subtle light, current, or motion that ripples across the matrix.
* Fabrics, hair, and dust motes move in correlated oscillations; when one panel shifts, the others subtly respond.
* **Medium:** Air with water-like viscosity (μ ≈ 0.0012 Pa·s)
* **Ambient Current:** 0.15–0.2 m/s oscillation shared across panels.
* **Lighting:** Kelvin sweep 5500K → 8200K; volumetric caustics move harmoniously across the four scenes.
* **Audio:** Unified soundscape — sub-1 kHz hum, faint heartbeat rhythm, soft rustle of fabric, underwater resonance — blending seamlessly across quadrants.
