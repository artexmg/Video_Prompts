TITLE: BELOW THE SURFACE

Length: 25 seconds
FPS: 30
Resolution: 8K
Aspect Ratio: 9:16
Style: Psychedelic · Hyper-Kinetic · Whimsical Predator
Model: SORA 2

CORE PHYSICAL RULES (KEEP VERBATIM — ABSOLUTE)

All entities have mass, inertia, and drag
Pond is viscous, elastic, load-bearing, semi-transparent
Every step displaces liquid → ripple → rebound
No hovering, no gliding, no frozen poses
Motion is continuous across cuts and split-screens
Water always resists motion before yielding
Camera motion is affected by drag and partial submersion

AUDIO CONSTRAINTS (CRITICAL)

No voices
No speech
No language
No human-like vocalizations

Sound design uses:
• viscous liquid suction
• elastic water recoil
• low-frequency pressure movement
• alien breathy bellows (non-rhythmic, animal-like)
• hunter sub-bass exhalations and internal clicks only

Music is textural pressure only — no melody, no rhythm, no cues

CHARACTERS (ENFORCED)
AMPHIBIOUS ALIEN HERD composed of around 7 or 8 creatures

Rounded, bulbous bodies with thick textured skin
Vivid pinks, magentas, neon greens
Raised nodules, bioluminescent-looking bumps
Multiple short tentacle-legs compress pond surface
Curved candy-striped horns oscillate elastically
Movement feels collective, tidal, reactive

HUNTERS (NON-HUMAN)

Not human in anatomy, gait, or posture
Digitigrade or semi-quadrupedal locomotion
Spears used as stabilizers as much as weapons
Forward-leaning, hinge-driven motion
Helmet is a sensor node — no face, no head tilts
Movement is coordinated, procedural, non-expressive
Hunters are one third (1/3) the size of the ALIENS

🔥 STRUCTURED SEQUENCE (25s)
ACT I — FALSE SERENITY (0–6s)

0.0–1.2s
Extreme macro — alien tentacle-leg compresses viscous pond
Liquid dimples, stretches, rebounds slowly
Camera partially submerged, drag visible

1.2–3.0s
Wide herd shot — aliens advance calmly
Horns oscillate subtly, not randomly
Ripples travel against herd direction

3.0–6.0s
Split-screen (Horizontal x2):
• macro skin deformation
• wide herd movement
Camera arcs left under resistance
No aggression — only pressure

ACT II — SYSTEMIC DISTURBANCE (6–11s)

6.0–7.5s
High-angle — asymmetrical herd movement; uneven spacing; no grid or radial symmetry. Individuals compress inward at different speeds, colliding, slipping, and re-spacing under environmental pressure. Camera drifts slightly, preserving organic chaos.
Water tension thickens between bodies

7.5–9.0s
Split-screen (3 panels):
• subsurface pressure waves
• horn oscillation phase-locking
• foam forming without impact

9.0–11.0s
Camera slows unnaturally as if dragged
Aliens subtly redirect movement
They are reacting — not hunting

ACT III — PREDATORS MATERIALIZE (11–17s)

11.0–12.5s
Hunters break surface already in motion
Not entering — revealing presence
Spears brace into pond, liquid bows around shafts

12.5–14.0s
Low water-level tracking
Hunters emerge behind herd elements
No charge, no roar — just alignment

14.0–17.0s
Over-the-shoulder alien POV
Hunters stabilize, angle spears
Pressure peaks — no strike yet

ACT IV — KINETIC COLLAPSE (17–25s)

17.0–18.5s

Aliens attempt retreat
Too late — mass works against them

18.5–19.5s
Hunters redirect, pin, destabilize
Spears embed at horn bases and flank seams
Water erupts, elastic recoil visible

19.5–21.5s
Low-angle close-up — dominant alien thrashes
Hunters cling using spear leverage
Camera buffeted by spray and drag

21.5–23.5s
Wide — alien rears once, loses buoyancy
Body sinks under its own mass
Hunters remain anchored, not triumphant

23.5–24.5s
Slow descent into viscous pond
Motion never stops 
OMINOUS FEELING AND FX

blur out the image under water drag and end with BIG and elegant glowing serif letters blooming softly: “BELOW THE SURFACE”
