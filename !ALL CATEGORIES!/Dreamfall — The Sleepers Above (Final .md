# Dreamfall — The Sleepers Above (Final Rewrite)

Format: 8K UHD • 24 fps • 15 s
Genre: Surreal realism / dream physics
Tone: Tranquil, sacred, intimate — between sleep and the deep sea
Camera: 35 mm equivalent, f/2.8, 1/200 s, ISO 400, WB 7500 K
Lighting: Kelvin sweep 5000 K → 8200 K, soft volumetric haze, dawn glow bloom

## Concept Summary

A quiet bedroom at dawn where two young women float just above their beds, their sheer dresses and hair drifting gently as if underwater — yet their bodies remain perfectly real, weighted, and human.
All motion feels like slowed time, with physics intact but softened by air resistance and dreamlike light.

## Global Physical Rules

Anatomy: Bodies remain perfectly human and anatomically correct. No limb elongation, fusion, or deformation.
Gravity: Reduced gravity (≈ 0.3 g). Motion obeys real-world inertia and drag.
Airflow: Gentle upward current (0.1 m/s) adds lift but never full buoyancy.
Hair & Cloth: Move separately; hair follows inertia lag ≈ 0.3 s, cloth lags ≈ 0.25 s, with independent flow vectors.
Interaction: No interpenetration — bodies, cloth, and environment remain physically distinct.
Light Behavior: Stable refraction and bloom; no distortion of geometry.
Motion Style: Time-dilated realism — every movement 10× slower than natural speed.
Camera Movement: Slow, steady, cinematic; uses parallax and shallow depth for immersion.

## Negative Prompt

No metallic or abstract shapes.
No surreal body warping, no melted or fluid anatomy.
No glowing outlines or non-physical effects.
No exaggerated underwater bubbles or particles beyond soft dust motes.
