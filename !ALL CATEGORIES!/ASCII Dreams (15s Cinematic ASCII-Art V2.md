ASCII Dreams (15s Cinematic ASCII-Art Video)

Visual Style

Entire video rendered as ASCII art, monochrome (white characters on black background).
Characters used: @ # % & / \ ( ) { } [ ] * + = - . , : ;
Density of characters represents shading and lighting (darker = denser characters).
Smooth interpolation — characters morph based on light and shadow, not blocky.
Motion blur simulated by character stretching (e.g., repeated === or /////).
Avoid retro CRT look unless needed; aim for elevated cinematic ASCII realism.

Camera

24 fps filmic motion
macro–wide alternation
dolly-ins, parallax shifts, slow pans
depth of field simulated through ANSI character density

Lighting
Strong contrast
ASCII “light rays” as streaks of repeating symbols
Shadows as clusters of # and %
Highlights as . and small whitespace pockets
