PAINT THAT LEARNED PHYSICS

Length: 25 seconds
Aspect Ratio: 9:16
FPS: 30
Quality: High-Def · Filmic · Macro Epic
Model: Sora 2

CORE PHYSICS CONSTRAINTS (KEEP VERBATIM)

All matter behaves as thick impasto paint with measurable mass
Paint viscosity is high (slow deformation, elastic rebound, no splashing mist)
Gravity is constant and directional across all frames
Spherical objects are solid, heavy, load-bearing
No floating, hovering, or weightless motion
Surface tension causes paint to stretch, compress, and recoil
Deformation is local (no global melting or morphing)
Motion is continuous, grounded, and physically causal

GLOBAL VISUAL STYLE

Macro-scale abstract environment resembling sculpted oil paint
Dense ridges, micro-bead granularity, palette-knife topology
Color world: deep teal, cyan, magenta, violet, gold particulate accents
Lighting is low-key cinematic, soft rim lights, shallow depth of field
Everything feels slow, heavy, intentional

STRUCTURAL MOTIF

The same scene is shown simultaneously from multiple angles using split screens.
All frames depict the same physical event, synchronized in time.

TIMELINE / STORYBOARD
0–3s | Emergence

Single frame, no split yet

Camera drifts across thick paint terrain
Micro-vibrations ripple through pigment ridges
Heavy spheres partially embedded, perfectly still
Subtle gravitational settling visible in surface compression

SFX:
Low sub-bass hum, distant, like pressure building

3–7s | First Contact

Vertical split — 2 panels

Left panel: side-angle macro view

Right panel: top-down macro view

One sphere slowly descends further into paint
Paint stretches upward before yielding
Compression rings propagate outward in slow waves

Physics clue: paint resists, then yields — not liquid, not solid

SFX:
Deep, rubbery low-frequency thud
Soft internal creak, like thick clay shifting

7–12s | Material Intelligence

Three-panel split (triptych)
Panel A: extreme close-up of paint grains deforming
Panel B: wide macro showing full interaction zone
Panel C: oblique angle catching specular highlights sliding across ridges

The sphere rebounds slightly
Paint recoils elastically, overshoots, then settles
Fine pigment dust shakes loose and resettles (no smoke, no mist)

FX:
Micro-shadows deepen inside stretched paint folds
Specular highlights move along ridges, revealing topology

SFX:
Wet, dense elasticity sounds
Sub-bass pulse synced to recoil

12–17s | Gravitational Dialogue

Horizontal split — 2 panels
Both panels show the same moment, different lenses:
Top: shallow DOF, creamy bokeh
Bottom: deep focus, structural clarity

Multiple spheres slowly drift inward due to gravity
Paint flows around them, never lifting them
Contact points glow subtly from friction and compression

Physics clue: motion always occurs through material resistance

SFX:
Slow rhythmic bass, heartbeat-like
Soft granular movement sounds

17–22s | Synchronized Collapse

Four-panel grid

All panels show different angles of a single event:

A larger sphere settles decisively
Paint displaces, ridges buckle, then lock into place
The entire environment stabilizes

No chaos. No explosion. Only resolution.

FX:
Micro-particles settle
Color saturation deepens as motion ceases

SFX:
Final heavy compression sound
Bass fades into silence

22–25s | End Card
Return to single frame
Camera slowly pulls back
The paint world is now still, balanced, complete
Text blooms softly from darkness (centered, elegant serif):
“Matter remembers”

Fade to black.

FINAL NOTES FOR SORA

Prioritize weight over speed
Prioritize resistance over flow
Avoid any smoke-like, gaseous, or fluid mist behavior
All split-screen frames must remain time-synchronized
The scene must feel physically inevitable, not animated
