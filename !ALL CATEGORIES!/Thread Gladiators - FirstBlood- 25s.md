25s STRUCTURE
SC0 — IMPACT OPEN / NO MERCY (0.0–1.3s)

Anchor image at frame 0, already in violent motion.
Camera is thrown laterally across the arena bowl.
Mounted gladiators are already charging around the curve.
Filament weapons are mid-swing.
Crowd is already convulsing in color turbulence.
A giant thread coil lashes across foreground and rips one fighter sideways out of saddle.
Yarn-dust hits lens immediately.

SC1 — LOW CHASE THROUGH THE KILL ZONE (1.3–3.5s)

Ground-level pursuit behind galloping horses.
Hooves punch into braided floor, making the arena surface buckle upward in strips.
A rider ducks beneath a whipping tendon-mass coming off the wall.
Another is clipped and spun off course.
Camera swerves between legs, shields, broken thread bundles, and dragged bodies.

SC2 — WALL BREACH / THE ARENA BITES BACK (3.5–6.0s)

A side wall tears open with no reveal pause.
Not a monster entrance — a structural wound.
From the rupture, a massive living filament mass violently extrudes and swings like a siege limb.
It does not pose. It hits immediately.
One blow caves part of the arena edge inward.
Crowd above breaks into cascading thread panic.
Camera whip-orbits, barely keeping the strike in frame.

SC3 — CLOSE-QUARTER ENTANGLEMENT (6.0–8.4s)
Fast brutal inserts:
sword jams into dense thread-muscle and sticks
shield gets wrapped and ripped away
horse skids as braided cords snare its forelegs
a gladiator is dragged face-first across the woven floor
another barely cuts free before being swallowed by collapsing strands

Everything is too close.
No relief wide shot.

SC4 — CROWD SHOCKWAVE CASCADE (8.4–10.8s)

Scale spike.
Camera lifts just enough to show the upper tiers reacting like a living fabric storm.
Thousands of micro-thread spectators ripple, slump, burst outward, and cascade down stairwells as structural strands recoil through the seating.
Flags whip violently.
Sections of woven balconies unzip and spill bodies and material into the bowl.
Arena becomes weather.

SC5 — CAVALRY COUNTER-SURGE (10.8–13.4s)

A surviving mounted group charges straight into the collapse zone.
Not heroic — desperate.
Camera tracks backward in front of them as they ride through hanging strands and falling debris.
A massive filament lash slams down beside them, blasting dust and bodies outward.
One rider vanishes behind a wall of red fiber spray.
Another leaps a tearing seam in the arena floor.

SC6 — TITANIC FLOOR FAILURE (13.4–16.0s)

The arena floor buckles in concentric waves.
Braided planks shear apart.
Whole lanes of combatants drop, slide, or are pitched sideways into exposed under-weave trenches.
A huge ochre strand erupts upward from below like a buried tendon under tension finally snapping free.
Camera plunges with the collapse, then yanks sideways to avoid impact.

SC7 — LIVING CANOPY DESCENT (16.0–18.7s)

Overhead filament canopy tears apart.
No grace.
No slow drift.
Entire hanging masses collapse as if a giant woven nervous system has failed.
Sunlight strobes through falling ropes, banners, dust, and bodies.
A partially formed thread titan is hit and folded under the avalanche but continues thrashing beneath it.
Camera retreats backward through raining debris.

SC8 — CENTER ARENA SLAUGHTER SPIRAL (18.7–21.2s)

Camera circles the central combat basin at high speed.
The fight has become non-directional chaos:
horses colliding
fighters cutting free from bindings
debris bouncing and dragging
giant wall-tendrils slamming into ground
crowd fragments pouring into the arena
dust making visibility intermittent
The arena now feels less like architecture and more like a wounded animal convulsing.

SC9 — FINAL OVERLOAD / NO VICTOR (21.2–24.8s)

Everything peaks together:
one last mounted charge through debris
wall mass lashes across frame
central floor tears open again
crowd shockwave rolls across the tiers
gold-white upper structure sheds huge braided chunks
red filament heaps surge across the arena floor like a flood

Camera is forced backward and upward, overwhelmed by total system failure.
No single winner.
No triumph beat.
Only the whole Colosseum consuming itself in motion.

At 24.8s: hard cut to dark.
End clean.
