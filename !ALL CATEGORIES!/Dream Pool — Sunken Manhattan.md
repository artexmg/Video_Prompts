A HyperRealistic 8K underwater short set in a crystal-clear, flooded Manhattan.
Two young women wander and laugh weightlessly through the sunken city as sunlight filters between skyscrapers, their movements governed by true fluid physics — buoyancy, cloth and hair drag, bubbles, and refraction.
The camera glides from a descent through the skyline to a radiant golden ascent, blending awe with serenity.

## General Description

### Environment:
Submerged Manhattan with turquoise light gradients, volumetric rays between towers, drifting debris, microbubbles, and caustic reflections off glass façades. Coral and algae trace the edges of old structures.

### Physics realism includes:
Fluid: clear urban floodwater, low turbidity (0.2 mg/L suspended silica/silt)
Buoyancy: near-neutral, vertical drift ≈ 0.07 m/s
Cloth: chiffon / light cotton, Cd ≈ 1.4, 200–400 ms lag, trapped-air release 2–3 s
Hair: low-Re flutter, curls align with eddies
Bubbles: 0.5–5 mm, rise 0.15–0.30 m/s, spiral detachment
Optics: Snell + Fresnel refraction; caustic cell 6–12 cm @ 0.3 m/s; chromatic dispersion (blue-green bias, red falloff)
Flow: 0.2–0.4 m/s street-canyon crossflow; vortices dissipate < 2 s
Lighting: attenuation gradient 5600 K→8500 K; volumetric caustics (≈20° cone)
Sound: low-pass < 1.5 kHz, bubble crackle, deep structural hum
