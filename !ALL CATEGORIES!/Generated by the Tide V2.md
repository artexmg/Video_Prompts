CORE CONSTRAINTS (CRITICAL — KEEP VERBATIM)

Orca anatomy must remain 100% realistic and correct at all times

No morphing, melting, or distortion of face, jaw, fins, spine, or tail

Motion must obey gravity, buoyancy, drag, and momentum

No floating, hovering, or sustained airborne suspension

Ink behaves as viscous, flowing liquid — never smoke, never mist

Waves are load-bearing, sculpted, elastic, and reactive

All motion feels intentional, cinematic, heavy, and physically grounded

CRITICAL WATER-BOUND MOTION CONSTRAINTS (ABSOLUTE)

The orca is ALWAYS interacting with ink-water mass

The body is never cleanly separated from the ocean

Any upward motion occurs THROUGH water, spray sheets, and liquid drag

Water visibly clings to the orca at all times: streaming, cascading, pulling it back

Motion reads as swimming, breaching, or carving through liquid — NEVER flying

Camera reinforces arcing or lateral motion, never vertical hero lift

GLOBAL STYLE / LEITMOTIF

A meta-creation sequence where the world draws itself into existence.

Everything is formed from flowing ink ribbons and engraved contour lines, like a living woodcut illustration animated by fluid motion.

Visual language:
Engraved lines · flowing filaments · liquid ink · glowing etched edges

Color evolution:
Monochrome ink → warm gold highlights → teal, cyan, coral ocean tones

High-contrast cinematic lighting, painterly but physically coherent.
