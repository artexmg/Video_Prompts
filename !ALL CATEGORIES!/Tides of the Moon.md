# Tides of the Moon

Format: HyperRealistic 8K • 24 fps • Cinematic surreal realism
Length: 15 seconds
Mood: Grand • Tragic • Serene

## Premise
The Moon has entered a decaying orbit. As it drifts closer, its gravitational pull tugs oceans skyward; air thickens with vapor and planktonic luminescence. Humanity drifts into silence, suspended in a world where gravity and tides merge into one breathless equilibrium.

## Scene Structure (≈ 1.5 – 2 s each)
Scene 1 — Lunar Descent (0 – 1.5 s)

Action: A colossal moon dominates the horizon above a motionless ocean.
Camera: Wide establishing shot; faint dolly push-in through mist.
Physics: Lunar diameter ≈ 10× normal; light intensity ≈ 6 lux; calm laminar surface (< 1 mm wave height).
Mood: Cosmic awe — the breath before the tide.

Scene 2 — The First Lift (1.5 – 3 s)

Action: The sea begins to rise; droplets hover mid-air.
Camera: Low-angle telephoto, waterline at frame center.
Physics: Effective gravity 0.85 g; droplets suspended by updraft ≈ 0.4 m/s.
Mood: Tension — nature pauses to inhale.

Scene 3 — City at the Shore (3 – 4.5 s)

Action: Silver water climbs glass towers; reflections ripple upward.
Camera: Drone-like orbital pan around skyline.
Physics: Sheet flow 0.1 m/s; refractive index 1.333 → mirrored distortion.
Mood: Civilization dissolving into reflection.

Scene 4 — Human Drift (4.5 – 6 s)

Action: People float weightlessly above flooded streets, arms outstretched.
Camera: Tracking medium shot; silhouettes rise through moonbeams.
Physics: Apparent g ≈ 0.4 g; buoyant mist density ≈ 1.4 kg/m³; cloth Cd ≈ 1.3.
Mood: Surrender — gravity becomes grace.

Scene 5 — The Silver Sky (6 – 8 s)

Action: Ocean curves upward into a dome, mirroring the Moon.
Camera: Tilt up; horizon bends concave; stars refract through humidity.
Physics: Tidal bulge radius > 100 km; refractive halo ≈ 20°.
Mood: Infinite intimacy — sky and sea entwine.

Scene 6 — Plankton Clouds (8 – 9.5 s)

Action: Air glows with drifting bioluminescent plankton.
Camera: Macro focus pull on particles; slow rotation.
Physics: Luminescence ≈ 3 cd/m²; drift velocity 0.2 m/s.
Mood: Living constellations — microscopic cosmos.

Scene 7 — The Touch (9.5 – 11 s)

Action: The Moon’s reflection meets the ocean — light erupts outward.
Camera: Extreme wide; flare expands to white.
Physics: Thermal vaporization ≈ 1000 K; plume 200 m/s; instantaneous corona.
Mood: Creation and collapse — one flash of eternity.

Scene 8 — Stillness (11 – 13 s)

Action: Everything halts — suspended water, frozen people, silent sky.
Camera: Locked frame; zero motion blur.
Physics: Net g = 0 m/s² (gravitational forces cancel).
Mood: Time stops — beauty crystallized.

Scene 9 — Reversal & Echo (13 – 15 s)

Action: Motion reverses; tides fall, the Moon rises, leaving only silver mist.
Camera: Pull-back to orbital distance; fade to black.
Physics: Reverse velocity mapping (–0.2 m/s); color temp 8200 K → 4800 K.
Mood: Quiet release — the universe exhales.
