Title: THE DOJO REMEMBERS
Duration: 25 seconds
FPS: 48 fps
Visual Style: high-contrast black & white · pointillist particle matter · dots behave as solid mass
Tone: mythic · violent · supernatural · boss-intro energy

GLOBAL RULES (LOCKED)
NO standing still — EVER
Nothing floats unless actively forced
NO static shots
NO slow awakening
EVERY scene < 2 seconds
CONSTANT motion (camera, environment, particles)
Particles = heavy granular matter (sand + steel), never smoke
Motion driven by force, inertia, shockwaves
HIGH contrast silhouettes at all times
Samurai is always advancing, dodging, striking, or repositioning
NO slow walk cycles — movement is purposeful and aggressive
Camera moves as violently as the action


AUDIO (GLOBAL)
Music: epic hybrid orchestral + industrial percussion
Tempo: 130–145 BPM
Bass: sub hits synced to blade movement & impacts
FX: metallic shrieks, sword resonance, air tearing, debris shear
Mix: LOUD, aggressive, theatrical, overpowering
