TIDAL VEIL — Sora2 Prompt
15 sec · 8K UHD · 24fps · 2.39:1
Reference Image: Coastal mountain city with monumental wave and hilltop statue

STYLE & VISUAL LANGUAGE

Hyper-detailed anime realism with sculptural Rio de Janeiro, Brazil as a miniature-city,  detail
Crisp ink-like edge definition on water, architecture, and foliage
Extreme macro-to-massive scale contrast (miniature city vs. god-scale wave)
Teal / turquoise ocean contrasted with warm gold, coral, and emerald land tones
Volumetric spray, filamented foam strands, refractive highlights
High-contrast cinematic grading — glowing highlights, deep shadow wells
No passive shots — every frame driven by force, gravity, and motion

AUDIO

Low-frequency sub-bass swell building beneath the scene
Wind howling through dense city blocks and mountain vegetation
Creaking boats, snapping cables, rattling metal structures
No music until final seconds — pure environmental tension
Wave impact roar syncs precisely with visual collapse

CAMERA LANGUAGE

Aggressive, energized movement only — zero static frames after anchor
Hybrid scale camera: miniature macro passes → colossal wide sweeps
Lens progression: 35mm → 18mm → 50mm → 12mm → 70mm macro → 24mm wide
Fast parallax exaggerates city scale and wave height
Occasional lens contamination: spray, droplets, refraction distortion

ENVIRONMENT & PHYSICS

Water behaves with immense mass and weight, not stylized floatiness
Foam fractures into hairline filaments and tangled tendrils
Vortices curl inside the wave wall; subsurface pressure visible
Boats strain against moorings, hulls pitching violently
Vegetation ripples in shockwaves from wind pressure
Clouds churn and shear as if pulled by the wave’s gravity

GOAL & TONE

Capture the moment just before annihilation —
overwhelming scale, terrifying beauty, divine indifference.
The city feels fragile, ornamental, and doomed beneath unstoppable nature.

SCENE 0 — ANCHOR SHOT (0.0–0.3s)

Camera: Locked wide establishing shot.

Visuals:
• The coastal city curves along the bay, dense with miniature buildings and boats
• The mountain rises steeply, crowned by the iconic outstretched statue
• The colossal wave looms offshore, frozen in coiled tension
• Micro-motions only: water surface ripples, foliage trembles, clouds billow subtly
• Sunlight glints across wave filaments and city edges

Hard whip cut at 0.3s.

SCENE 1 — THE PULLBACK (0.3–2.0s)

Camera: Fast dolly backward along the shoreline road.

Action:
• The wave grows taller through parallax, dwarfing buildings
• Spray blasts past the lens from offscreen wind shear
• Shadows from clouds race across the city facade
• Boats buck violently in the harbor

FX: Rattling metal, wind scream, lens speckled with droplets.

SCENE 2 — SUBSURFACE SURGE (2.0–4.0s)

Camera: Plunges beneath the waterline into the wave’s interior.

Action:
• Underwater currents twist violently around debris and rocks
• Teal light shafts pierce bubble clouds
• Foam trails stretch upward toward the surface like pulled threads

Mood: Crushing pressure, unstoppable momentum.

SCENE 3 — STREET-LEVEL RUSH (4.0–6.0s)

Camera: Handheld, waist-height, sprinting through a narrow city street.

Action:
• Power lines whip overhead
• Banners and signs tear violently in the wind
• Dust, leaves, and water droplets slam into the lens
• Buildings rush past with extreme parallax

FX: Intense camera shake, breathless motion, screaming wind.

SCENE 4 — OVER-THE-ROOF SWEEP (6.0–8.0s)

Camera: Drone-like glide over rooftops toward the mountain.

Action:
• The wave’s shadow engulfs the city block by block
• The statue stands calm and immovable against chaos
• Foamy tendrils begin curling at the wave crest
• Sunlight streaks along the water wall like molten gold

SCENE 5 — THE BREAK (8.0–11.0s)

Camera: Violent tilt-up from street level to the collapsing crest.

Action:
• The wave folds forward, towering and immense
• Water fractures into millions of micro-filaments
• Boats, debris, and spray suspend midair for a heartbeat
• The statue is briefly framed against the collapsing water mass

Sound: Sub-bass peaks — the roar becomes overwhelming.

SCENE 6 — WHITEOUT IMPACT (11.0–14.5s)

Action:

• Lens completely drenched, frame vibrating
