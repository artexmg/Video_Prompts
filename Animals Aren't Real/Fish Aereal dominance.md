TITLE: FISH AERIAL DOMINANCE
FORMAT
Duration: 25 seconds
FPS: 48fps
Aspect Ratio: 9:16 (Vertical)
Layout: Vertical Split Screen (Top / Bottom)
Color: muted industrial palette + oxidized reds / teal water
Tone: ritualistic · invasive · hostile intelligence

GLOBAL HARD RULES (LOCKED)
NO static shots — EVER
NO scene longer than 1.6s
Motion is continuous or violently interrupted
Camera instability is intentional
Time does NOT flow smoothly — it chokes
Sound design locks to motion & cuts
Bird and Submarine operate on independent physics + tempo

SPLIT-SCREEN LOGIC

HORIZONTAL DIAGONAL DOMINANCE SPLIT (≈30° TILT)
Think:
AERIAL DOMINATES
UNDERWATER IS BURDENED
Gravity encoded into the frame

STRUCTURE SPEC
Frame is divided by a diagonal horizon line (~30° tilt):

TOP ZONE (≈60–65% of frame)
→ Mechanical bird
→ Air, speed, violence, nervous energy

BOTTOM ZONE (≈35–40% of frame)
→ Submarine / torpedo body
→ Water, pressure, inertia, mass
⚠️ The diagonal is NOT clean:
It jitters
It slips 1–3% over time
It occasionally “tears” during time-stutter

TOP (BIRD — AERIAL)
Always above
Faster clock
Shorter cuts
Erratic, predatory motion
Breaks frame boundaries occasionally
Motion ignores gravity logic

BOTTOM (SUB — AQUATIC)
Always below
Slower inertia
Heavier sound design
Obeys drag, pressure, resistance
Never fully catches up

They are linked, but never synchronized; tension comes from desync.

AMERA LANGUAGE UPDATE
Camera horizon is tilted by design
No level shots — EVER
During impacts:
diagonal angle briefly over-rotates (+5°)
then snaps back with a frame drop
Lens breathing visible at the diagonal boundary

AUDIO (GLOBAL)
Genre: Dark PHONK
BPM: 135–145
Elements:
Distorted cowbell hits (scene cuts)
Sub-bass growls synced to wing snaps
Tape saturation + vinyl crackle
Sonar pings used as rhythmic fills
Hard cuts = audio dropouts (2–3 frames)

AUDIO SYNC
Bird actions trigger:
cowbell hits
tape glitches
high-mid distortions

Submarine actions trigger:
sub-bass hits
sonar thumps
low-frequency warble
⚠️ Audio pans subtly top-heavy, reinforcing dominance.
