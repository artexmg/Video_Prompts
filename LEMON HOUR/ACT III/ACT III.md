

## ACT III — THE VISIONS EXPAND

**2:00–3:15 — 5 core beats**

This is where the film opens beyond the apartment. Use this act to earn the 5-minute length. The lemon visions allow you to show different places and futures.

### Beat 9 — The appartment fractures

**2:00–2:15**

The girls stand around the table. Lemons are arranged in a rough circle, almost accidentally. 

The camera moves around them. Each time the camera passes behind a candle flame, the room briefly changes in a kinetic montage.
: 

old apartment, 
Morning apartment, 
Flooded apartment, 
Lemon-tree room.
Warm apartment.
Abandoned apartment.
Morning apartment.
Abandoned apartment.
A version full of dead plants.
A version with lemons covering the floor.

The apartment is no longer just a place. It is becoming a memory machine.

Visual goal: the apartment becomes unstable.

Seedance options:
Circular table ritual shot. Multiple versions of the apartment. Candle wipe transitions. Lemon circle close-up.

### Beat 10 — Mina’s first real vision: the train station

**2:15–2:30**

Mina looks through the lemon again. She sees herself at an empty train station before dawn, suitcase beside her. No friends is there to say goodbye. Rain shines on the platform. The announcement speaker crackles but says nothing.

A train arrives, but every window reflects the apartment party: Samantha laughing, Becca filming, Mina holding lemons over her eyes.

Mina reaches toward the train door, but inside she sees the older version of herself watching.

Mina’s fear is clear: leaving may turn her into someone unreachable.

Seedance options:
Empty train station with Mina. Suitcase close-up. Train windows reflecting party. Older Mina inside train.

### Beat 11 — Blonde friend’s expanded vision: the performance room

**2:30–2:45**

Samantha sees herself in a bright performance, glamorous room full of people clapping for her. She is glowing, beautiful, adored. Funny. Needed.

But when she stops smiling, the whole room goes silent, everyone’s faces are blank. The audience waits.

She tries to speak honestly, but no sound comes out. They only clap when she smiles.

She stops smiling. The room goes silent. The lights go out one by one.The spotlight becomes harsh. Her smile fades.
 

Samantha’s fear is not loneliness exactly. It is being loved only when she is entertaining. She is tired of performing happiness.

Seedance options:
Samantha in party spotlight. Applause with blank faces. Close-up smile fading. Lights going out.

### Beat 12 — Becca's expanded vision: the argument that never ends

**2:45–3:00**

Becca sees a hallway that repeats forever.

Mina stands at the far end. Every time Becca tries to reach her, a door closes. 

Becca opens one door and finds the same hallway again. Same Mina, same distance.

On the wall: framed photos of the three friends, but in each photo one of them is missing.

Becca’s fear is control. She would rather act like she needs no one than admit someone can hurt her. She avoids vulnerability until the door closes.

Seedance options:
Repeating hallway. Mina at far end. Door closing. Photos with missing friend.

### Beat 13 — Old Mina appears in their visions

**3:00–3:15**

All three girls now see the same thing: Old Mina sitting at their table. She has long dark hair, bangs, and a calm face, serene. She is not frightening. Almost sad. She is older. 

She holds a single lemon in one hand.

She does not threaten them. She waits.

Back in the apartment, one chair at the table is suddenly pulled out.

Samantha stops smiling.

Becca whispers, “Did you see her too?”

Mina says nothing.

Seedance options:
Older Mina at table. Empty chair pulled out. Group staring at chair. Lemon glowing faintly in hand.
