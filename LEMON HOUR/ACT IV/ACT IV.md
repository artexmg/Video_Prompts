## ACT IV — THE CONFESSION RITUAL

**3:15–4:15 — 4 core beats**

This act should slow down emotionally, but not become visually dead. Keep hands, lemons, candles, faces, reflections, glasses, shadows alive. This is where the short film becomes human instead of just magical.

### Beat 14 — Mina understands

**3:15–3:30**

Mina realizes the truth.

Mina says “The lemons aren’t showing the future,”. She continues  “They’re showing what we’re afraid will make the future happen.”

The room becomes very quiet.

Mina cuts a lemon open. The knife sound feels unnaturally loud. Juice runs over the table like gold.

Visual goal: The party is over. The ritual has begun.

Seedance options:
Mina speaking quietly. Knife cutting lemon. Lemon juice across table. Samantha and Becca listening in silence.

### Beat 15 — Blonde confession

**3:30–3:45**

Samantha squeezes lemon juice into a glass. 
Samantha tries to joke first, but can’t. 
Then, Samantha says: “I’m scared that if I stop being fun, no one will stay.”
For once, Samantha does not cover it with a smile.

Mina reaches for her hand.

Becca looks away because it hits too close.

The candles behind Samantha burn brighter for a moment.

Seedance options:
Samantha squeezing lemon. Close-up of her face trying not to cry. Mina taking her hand. Candle flare.

### Beat 16 —  Becca’s confession

**3:45–4:00**

Becca takes the glass.

She says: “I act like I don’t need anyone because needing people feels like giving them a weapon.”

She looks direclty at Mina. This is the first fully honest thing Becca has said all night.

A framed photo on the wall changes: now all three girls are visible together again.

Seedance options:
Becca holding glass. Honest eye contact with Mina. Frame/photo changing subtly. Group of Becca, Mina, and Samantha around table.

### Beat 17 — Mina confession

**4:00–4:15**

Mina finally drinks from the glass.

She says: “I said I was leaving because I wanted to become someone else. But I think I was just afraid you already knew who I was.”

Samantha starts crying quietly.

Becca reaches for Mina’s other hand.

The room goes darker. One by one, the candles blow out. Not violently. Gently. Not like a jump scare. Like the room is exhaling.

At the final candle, complete darkness.

Seedance options:
Mina drinking lemon juice. Mina confession close-up. Candles extinguishing. Full darkness transition.
