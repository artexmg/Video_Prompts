
## ACT II — THE GAME BECOMES REAL

**1:00–2:00 — 4 core beats**

This act is where the party turns into a dare. The rules of the lemon begin to appear, but nobody fully understands them.

### Beat 5 — Becca Says the rule 

**1:00–1:15**
Becca picks up a lemon and says,  half-joking: “They say if you look through a lemon after midnight, you see the truth.”

Samantha immediately mocks her: “That sounds like something invented by a drunk aunt.”

They laugh, but the candle flames flicker in sync. The wall clock shows midnight, or maybe 12:01.

Mina looks at the lemons. She does not laugh this time.

Visual goal: still playful, but the room listens.

Seedance options:
Close-up of clock at midnight. Candle flames flickering. Becca delivering the line. Mina watching the lemons.

### Beat 6 — Samantha's first vision

**1:15–1:30**

Samantha tries it first, because of course she does.She looks through the lemon and sees herself years later, sitting in the same room, dressed beautifully. Everyone is laughing at her jokes. Everyone is clapping. She is glowing.

Then the applause stops.

Every face in the room goes blank. Nobody loves her. They are only waiting for her to perform again.

Then the vision cuts to Samantha alone in the kitchen, washing lipstick from a glass, still smiling while crying.

Samantha pulls the lemon away and says, “Okay. Weird lighting.” But her smile is fake now.

Samantha's fear: being loved only as entertainment.

Seedance options:
lemon-eye close-up. Future party version of her laughing. Alone kitchen sink shot. Samantha's fake smile returning.

### Beat 7 — Becca’s first vision

**1:30–1:45**

Becca takes the lemon next. She sees a future version of herself leaving the apartment in anger. She slams the door. Behind her, Mina and Samantha stand frozen, hurt but silent.

Then Becca sees a phone screen full of old unread messages from Mina. Months have passed. Maybe years.

She lowers the lemon, suddenly less amused.

Samantha asks, “What did you see?”

Becca says, “Nothing.”

Mina knows that is a lie.

Beccas' fear: pride causing permanent distance.

Seedance options:
Becca holding lemon slice. Door slam. Phone screen with unread messages, no readable text needed. Becca's face tightening.

### Beat 8 — Mina refuses to look again

**1:45–2:00**

Samantha and Becca pressure Mina to try again, but she refuses. Mina says she already looked. The mood gets weird. Becca watches the table.


Samantha tries to force the party back into fun. Music louder. More dancing. More lemon jokes. More filming.

But now the room behaves strangely. Lemons roll across the table by themselves. In the background, the older Mina silhouette appears for one frame in the doorway.

Then she is gone.

End Act II with the first clear “we are in something now” feeling.

Seedance options:
Mina backing away from lemon. Lemons rolling on table. Becca and Samantha dancing too hard. Doorway silhouette of Older Mina.
