
## ACT V — OLDER MINA / THE CHOICE

**4:15–5:00 — 3 core beats**

This final act must not over-explain. Let it feel mythic but intimate. Older Mina is not a ghost, not a monster, not exactly time travel. She is a possible self. A witness. A consequence.

### Beat 18 — Older Mina arrives

**4:15–4:30**

The lights return, but colder and quieter.

Older Mina sits at the table in the empty chair. She looks like Mina, but older: calmer, wiser, carrying the sadness of many choices and the peace of surviving them.

Older Mina holds the whole lemon, bright, in one hand.

The girls are frozen. She looks mostly at Mina, not the others.

She looks at Mina and says: “Good. You changed it.”

No one asks what she means. That makes it stronger.

Seedance options:
Lights returning. Older Mina revealed at table. Mina staring at her older self. Whole, bright lemon in Older Mina’s hand.

### Beat 19 — The room resets / morning

**4:30–4:45**

Cut to morning.

The apartment is bright and soft.The room looks normal again.

The party is over. Lemons everywhere. 

Samantha sleeps curled on the couch. Becca is asleep on the floor near the table. 

Mina wakes up in a chair, still holding part of a lemon.

Her suitcase is still by the door. 

Her phone alarm rings.

She looks at the suitcase, the ticket. Then at Samantha. Then at Becca.

Visual goal: quiet aftermath, emotional clarity.

Seedance options:
Morning apartment wide shot. Friends asleep among lemons. Mina waking up. Suitcase by door.

### Beat 20 — Final choice

**4:45–5:00**

Mina picks up a lemon. 

She does not leave yet. 

She holds the lemon to one eye and looks directly into camera.

For a second, we see what she sees: not a fixed future, but branching glimpses kinetically montage:

Mina leaving.
Mina staying.
Mina returning.
Mina older.
Mina alone.
Mina laughing with Samantha and Becca.
Mina crying.
Mina free.
All possible.

Not destiny.

A choice.


Then back to Mina’s face.

She smiles — not because everything is solved, but because now she knows it is a choice.

Cut to black.

Final sound: a lemon rolling gently across the table.

Seedance options:
Mina holding lemon to eye. Branching future montage. Mina smile close-up. Lemon rolling on table.
