Beat 1 — Arrival at the Apartment

Total duration: 15 seconds
Location tags: @street_exterior, @stairwell, @living_room
Characters: @Mina: @Mina, @Samantha: @Samantha, @Becca: @Becca
Tone: warm, intimate, realistic, handheld, social, memory-like, slightly cinematic but not supernatural yet.
Story purpose: establish the apartment, the night, the friendship, and the cozy farewell-party atmosphere. This is the normal world. Lemons should not dominate yet.

Seedance 2.0 Prompt — Beat 1

@street_exterior, @stairwell, @living_room. Night. A warm old apartment glows above a dark quiet street. Inside the windows: candlelight, plants, fairy lights, old wood, soft movement. This is the beginning of a farewell night for @Mina with her closest friends, @Samantha and @Becca. The scene should feel intimate, social, realistic, warm, and slightly nostalgic, like memory before anything strange happens. No overt supernatural elements yet. Minimal lemon presence only, not emphasized.

0.00–2.50 — Exterior establishing shot, cinematic handheld drift.
Start outside on a dark residential street. The camera looks up toward a warmly lit apartment window glowing amber against the blue-black night. Through the window, we see soft movement and silhouettes of three young women inside, laughing. A few plants are visible near the window, and warm fairy-light bokeh flickers in the glass. Camera slowly pushes inward from the street, natural handheld motion, realistic low-light texture. Lens: 35mm, slight grain, shallow but not extreme depth of field.

2.50–5.00 — Arrival energy at the entrance / stairs.
Cut to the stairwell or apartment landing. Quick intimate handheld fragments: footsteps on old stairs, a hand brushing the railing, a jacket sleeve, a burst of laughter as @Samantha enters first with high energy, turning back toward @Mina and @Becca. @Samantha feels bright and playful. @Becca follows, calmer, observant, carrying herself more quietly. @Mina arrives with a soft smile, slightly distracted beneath the warmth. Lens: 28mm handheld, close proximity, natural motion blur, quick lived-in movement.

5.00–8.00 — Entering the apartment, domestic detail montage.
Cut inside the apartment. Close fragments: shoes by the door, keys dropped onto a wooden entry table, a coat tossed over a chair, candles being lit, fairy lights glowing in a cabinet reflection, soft shadows moving across plants and walls. The apartment should feel loved-in, cozy, and beautiful without feeling staged. Old wood, amber light, green plants, soft practical lights. A small bowl of citrus or fruit may be glimpsed somewhere in the kitchen background, but it should not be highlighted yet. Lens: 50mm macro-feel inserts, then quick handheld transitions.

8.00–11.00 — Mina crosses the room, quiet emotional anchor.
The camera finds @Mina moving through the living room, smiling as she passes through the warm light. She exchanges a glance with @Samantha, then with @Becca. She is clearly loved, but there is something slightly inward about her, as if part of her is already thinking about tomorrow. Keep this subtle. No suitcase emphasis yet unless it naturally soft-blurs in the background for a moment. Camera tracks beside her in a medium shot. Lens: 50mm, shallow focus, warm skin tones, soft candle glow on her face.

11.00–13.00 — Group energy, friendship established.
@Samantha moves into frame and playfully bumps shoulders with @Mina, pulling her farther into the room. @Becca lingers just behind, smiling quietly as she watches them. The three women settle naturally into the space, laughing and speaking over one another. This should feel effortless and lived-in, like close friends slipping into a familiar ritual of being together. Camera circles lightly around them in handheld medium-wide coverage. Lens: 35mm.

13.00–15.00 — Ending beat: the apartment as memory.
End on a warm, intimate living-room composition: @Mina in foreground or midground, @Samantha moving animatedly nearby, @Becca glancing around the room, candles flickering, plants framing the shot, fairy lights reflecting in glass. Hold just long enough for the viewer to feel the room as a safe emotional container. Cut before anything strange happens.
