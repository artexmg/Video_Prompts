Seedance 2.0 Prompt — Beat 3

@living_room, @kitchen. Night. Warm old apartment, amber candles, fairy lights, green plants, old wood, bowls of lemons on the table, cozy farewell-party atmosphere. @Mina, @Samantha, and @Becca are in the living room near the kitchen entrance. The scene is handheld, intimate, realistic, cinematic, low-light, shallow depth of field, soft warm bokeh. This is still playful and social, not openly supernatural yet.

0.00–2.00 — Handheld party energy, wide-medium take.
Camera starts close on a bowl of lemons on the table, then whip-pans up to @Samantha grabbing @Mina by the shoulders and pulling her into frame. @Samantha is blonde, playful, bright, teasing. @Mina laughs, slightly shy, trying to resist but enjoying it. @Becca stands nearby holding her phone vertically, already recording. Lens: 28mm handheld, slight natural shake, warm candle glow, realistic motion blur.

2.00–4.50 — Fake farewell video begins, phone-recording setup.
@Samantha points dramatically at @Becca’s phone and says silently with exaggerated mouth movement, as if directing a fake documentary: “Record this.” She grabs two lemons from the table and presses them into @Mina’s hands like props, but nobody uses them as goggles yet. @Mina laughs and gives a tiny fake bow, playing along. @Becca smirks behind the phone, amused but calmer and more observant. Camera alternates between real camera view and quick insert of @Becca’s phone screen recording them.

4.50–7.50 — Mina’s smile flickers.
@Samantha performs an overly dramatic goodbye, gesturing toward @Mina’s suitcase half-hidden near the door in the background. @Mina laughs, then her smile briefly weakens when the leaving becomes real. She glances toward the suitcase for less than a second. @Samantha does not notice. @Becca notices through the phone screen. Take type: intimate handheld medium close-up, 50mm lens feel, shallow focus on @Mina’s face, background suitcase softly blurred.

7.50–10.50 — Phone screen catches the impossible detail.
Insert shot: @Becca’s phone screen shows @Mina and @Samantha laughing in the living room. In the phone screen only, behind them near the dark hallway/kitchen threshold, a fourth woman-shaped figure stands still for one second. The figure resembles @Old_Mina but is indistinct: older, calm, long dark hair, barely lit by candle spill. In the real room behind the phone, the hallway is empty. This must be subtle, not a jump scare. No horror pose. Just a quiet impossible presence.

10.50–12.50 — Becca reacts silently.
@Becca lowers the phone slightly and looks toward the hallway. Rack focus from the phone screen to @Becca’s eyes. Her smile fades. She checks the real hallway. Nothing there. @Samantha keeps joking in the foreground, still making @Mina hold lemons like props. @Mina is laughing again, unaware. Camera: 70mm close-up feel on @Becca, soft breathing focus, warm low light.

12.50–15.00 — Return to playful surface, hidden unease.
@Samantha pulls @Mina closer for the fake farewell video. @Mina laughs and holds the lemons up near her chest like a silly award. @Becca raises the phone again, but now she is unsettled. The final frame holds on @Becca’s phone recording @Mina and @Samantha — this time the hallway behind them is empty. A candle flickers once. Cut before anyone explains.

Visual rules: realistic human faces, natural anatomy, no exaggerated horror, no fantasy glow, no cartoon look, no text, no subtitles, no visible UI except the believable phone camera view. The supernatural clue should be subtle and easy to miss. Keep the scene warm, intimate, playful, and social, with only a faint wrongness under the surface.
