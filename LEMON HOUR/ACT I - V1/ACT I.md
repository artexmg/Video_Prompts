Working title: **THE LEMON HOUR**

CHARACTERS:

Mina as the main character, Samantha as the blonde friend, Becca as the brunette friend, and Old Mina as the future/possible self.

The film should feel like: **warm party → playful ritual → impossible visions → emotional confession → time folds → morning choice.**

The lemons should start as a joke, then become a lens, then become a ritual object, then finally become a symbol of choice.

## ACT I — THE PARTY / NORMAL WORLD

**0:00–1:00 — 4 core beats**

This act should be charming, social, warm, almost too alive. The supernatural should not be obvious yet. Just tiny details that feel “off” if someone rewatches.

### Beat 1 — Arrival at the apartment

**0:00–0:15**

Night exterior. A warm old apartment window glows above a dark street. Through the glass, we see plants, candles, movement, three girls laughing. Someone carries a paper bag of lemons up the stairs.

Inside, quick fragments: keys dropped on a wooden table, lemon bag spilling open, candles being lit, Mina smiling as she moves through the room.

Visual goal: establish the place as intimate, warm, slightly magical.

Seedance options to generate:
One exterior-to-window shot. One close-up of lemons spilling on table. One handheld interior shot of Mina crossing frame.

### Beat 2 — Mina’s farewell party

**0:15–0:30**
Mina is surrounded by her two closest friends: Samantha and Becca.

Mina is the center, but not in a loud way. She is smiling, but there’s a sadness underneath. Her suitcase is visible somewhere in the background, half-hidden near the door. Her friends tease her, hug her, take photos.

Samantha, the blonde friend, is playful, bright, teasing, always trying to keep the mood alive. She makes the party feel safe and funny.

Becca, the brunette friend, is calmer and sharper. She watches more than she performs. She notices Mina’s suitcase near the door before anyone says anything.

Mina is warm, but distracted. She laughs with them, but every time the camera catches her alone, we see the truth: she is leaving tomorrow, and she is not sure whether she is brave or running away.

Important visual: the suitcase appears only for a second, not emphasized too much.

Seedance options:
Group laughing with lemons. Mina near suitcase. Blonde friend handing Mina a drink or lemon. Brunette watching Mina quietly.

### Beat 3 — The lemon joke begins

**0:30–0:45**

Samantha picks up two lemon halves and puts them over her eyes, like ridiculous googles.

Everyone laughs. Mina copies her. Becca films it on her phone,  amused but pretending not to be. Samantha poses dramatically, Mina laughs harder than expected, and the party briefly feels perfect.

This should be playful, messy, alive. The lemons are just silly props. But during one fast camera movement, a reflection in the cabinet glass shows four girls in the room instead of three.

Nobody notices.

Seedance options:
Mina with lemon goggles. Blonde friend laughing with lemon. Phone POV recording. Cabinet reflection with a subtle extra silhouette.

### Beat 4 — The first strange detail

**0:45–1:00**

Mina holds one lemon half to one eye and looks around the room. For one split second, the warm room becomes cold and empty. Same furniture, but dusty. Dead plants. No girls. No music. One candle burns blue and one lemon sits on the table, fresh and bright.

Mina drops the lemon and laughs it off. The others think she’s playing.

Samantha laughs with her. Becca notices Mina’s face.

End Act I with a tiny tonal shift: the music or sound drops for half a second.

Seedance options:
Mina looking through lemon. Empty future apartment. Lemon falling onto table. Friends laughing while Mina looks unsettled.
