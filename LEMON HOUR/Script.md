Yes. Since you’ll generate **more than 20 clips**, I’d design this as a **5-minute film with 20 “story beats,”** but each beat can have **2–4 Seedance generations** so you have options in DaVinci.

Working title: **THE LEMON HOUR**

Characters: 
Mina as the main character, Samantha as the blonde friend, Becca as the brunette friend, and Old Mina as the future/possible self.

The film should feel like: **warm party → playful ritual → impossible visions → emotional confession → time folds → morning choice.**

The lemons should start as a joke, then become a lens, then become a ritual object, then finally become a symbol of choice.

## ACT I — THE PARTY / NORMAL WORLD

**0:00–1:00 — 4 core beats**

This act should be charming, social, warm, almost too alive. The supernatural should not be obvious yet. Just tiny details that feel “off” if someone rewatches.

### Beat 1 — Arrival at the apartment

**0:00–0:15**

Night exterior. A warm old apartment window glows above a dark street. Through the glass, we see plants, candles, movement, three girls laughing. Someone carries a paper bag of lemons up the stairs.

Inside, quick fragments: keys dropped on a wooden table, lemon bag spilling open, candles being lit, Mina smiling as she moves through the room.

Visual goal: establish the place as intimate, warm, slightly magical.

Seedance options to generate:
One exterior-to-window shot. One close-up of lemons spilling on table. One handheld interior shot of Mina crossing frame.

### Beat 2 — Mina’s farewell party

**0:15–0:30**
Mina is surrounded by her two closest friends: Samantha and Becca.

Mina is the center, but not in a loud way. She is smiling, but there’s a sadness underneath. Her suitcase is visible somewhere in the background, half-hidden near the door. Her friends tease her, hug her, take photos.

Samantha, the blonde friend, is playful, bright, teasing, always trying to keep the mood alive. She makes the party feel safe and funny.

Becca, the brunette friend, is calmer and sharper. She watches more than she performs. She notices Mina’s suitcase near the door before anyone says anything.

Mina is warm, but distracted. She laughs with them, but every time the camera catches her alone, we see the truth: she is leaving tomorrow, and she is not sure whether she is brave or running away.

Important visual: the suitcase appears only for a second, not emphasized too much.

Seedance options:
Group laughing with lemons. Mina near suitcase. Blonde friend handing Mina a drink or lemon. Brunette watching Mina quietly.

### Beat 3 — The lemon joke begins

**0:30–0:45**

Samantha picks up two lemon halves and puts them over her eyes, like ridiculous googles.

Everyone laughs. Mina copies her. Becca films it on her phone,  amused but pretending not to be. Samantha poses dramatically, Mina laughs harder than expected, and the party briefly feels perfect.

This should be playful, messy, alive. The lemons are just silly props. But during one fast camera movement, a reflection in the cabinet glass shows four girls in the room instead of three.

Nobody notices.

Seedance options:
Mina with lemon goggles. Blonde friend laughing with lemon. Phone POV recording. Cabinet reflection with a subtle extra silhouette.

### Beat 4 — The first strange detail

**0:45–1:00**

Mina holds one lemon half to one eye and looks around the room. For one split second, the warm room becomes cold and empty. Same furniture, but dusty. Dead plants. No girls. No music. One candle burns blue and one lemon sits on the table, fresh and bright.

Mina drops the lemon and laughs it off. The others think she’s playing.

Samantha laughs with her. Becca notices Mina’s face.

End Act I with a tiny tonal shift: the music or sound drops for half a second.

Seedance options:
Mina looking through lemon. Empty future apartment. Lemon falling onto table. Friends laughing while Mina looks unsettled.

## ACT II — THE GAME BECOMES REAL

**1:00–2:00 — 4 core beats**

This act is where the party turns into a dare. The rules of the lemon begin to appear, but nobody fully understands them.

### Beat 5 — Becca Says the rule 

**1:00–1:15**
Becca picks up a lemon and says,  half-joking: “They say if you look through a lemon after midnight, you see the truth.”

Samantha immediately mocks her: “That sounds like something invented by a drunk aunt.”

They laugh, but the candle flames flicker in sync. The wall clock shows midnight, or maybe 12:01.

Mina looks at the lemons. She does not laugh this time.

Visual goal: still playful, but the room listens.

Seedance options:
Close-up of clock at midnight. Candle flames flickering. Becca delivering the line. Mina watching the lemons.

### Beat 6 — Samantha's first vision

**1:15–1:30**

Samantha tries it first, because of course she does.She looks through the lemon and sees herself years later, sitting in the same room, dressed beautifully. Everyone is laughing at her jokes. Everyone is clapping. She is glowing.

Then the applause stops.

Every face in the room goes blank. Nobody loves her. They are only waiting for her to perform again.

Then the vision cuts to Samantha alone in the kitchen, washing lipstick from a glass, still smiling while crying.

Samantha pulls the lemon away and says, “Okay. Weird lighting.” But her smile is fake now.

Samantha's fear: being loved only as entertainment.

Seedance options:
lemon-eye close-up. Future party version of her laughing. Alone kitchen sink shot. Samantha's fake smile returning.

### Beat 7 — Becca’s first vision

**1:30–1:45**

Becca takes the lemon next. She sees a future version of herself leaving the apartment in anger. She slams the door. Behind her, Mina and Samantha stand frozen, hurt but silent.

Then Becca sees a phone screen full of old unread messages from Mina. Months have passed. Maybe years.

She lowers the lemon, suddenly less amused.

Samantha asks, “What did you see?”

Becca says, “Nothing.”

Mina knows that is a lie.

Beccas' fear: pride causing permanent distance.

Seedance options:
Becca holding lemon slice. Door slam. Phone screen with unread messages, no readable text needed. Becca's face tightening.

### Beat 8 — Mina refuses to look again

**1:45–2:00**

Samantha and Becca pressure Mina to try again, but she refuses. Mina says she already looked. The mood gets weird. Becca watches the table.


Samantha tries to force the party back into fun. Music louder. More dancing. More lemon jokes. More filming.

But now the room behaves strangely. Lemons roll across the table by themselves. In the background, the older Mina silhouette appears for one frame in the doorway.

Then she is gone.

End Act II with the first clear “we are in something now” feeling.

Seedance options:
Mina backing away from lemon. Lemons rolling on table. Becca and Samantha dancing too hard. Doorway silhouette of Older Mina.

## ACT III — THE VISIONS EXPAND

**2:00–3:15 — 5 core beats**

This is where the film opens beyond the apartment. Use this act to earn the 5-minute length. The lemon visions allow you to show different places and futures.

### Beat 9 — The appartment fractures

**2:00–2:15**

The girls stand around the table. Lemons are arranged in a rough circle, almost accidentally. 

The camera moves around them. Each time the camera passes behind a candle flame, the room briefly changes in a kinetic montage.
: 

old apartment, 
Morning apartment, 
Flooded apartment, 
Lemon-tree room.
Warm apartment.
Abandoned apartment.
Morning apartment.
Abandoned apartment.
A version full of dead plants.
A version with lemons covering the floor.

The apartment is no longer just a place. It is becoming a memory machine.

Visual goal: the apartment becomes unstable.

Seedance options:
Circular table ritual shot. Multiple versions of the apartment. Candle wipe transitions. Lemon circle close-up.

### Beat 10 — Mina’s first real vision: the train station

**2:15–2:30**

Mina looks through the lemon again. She sees herself at an empty train station before dawn, suitcase beside her. No friends is there to say goodbye. Rain shines on the platform. The announcement speaker crackles but says nothing.

A train arrives, but every window reflects the apartment party: Samantha laughing, Becca filming, Mina holding lemons over her eyes.

Mina reaches toward the train door, but inside she sees the older version of herself watching.

Mina’s fear is clear: leaving may turn her into someone unreachable.

Seedance options:
Empty train station with Mina. Suitcase close-up. Train windows reflecting party. Older Mina inside train.

### Beat 11 — Blonde friend’s expanded vision: the performance room

**2:30–2:45**

Samantha sees herself in a bright performance, glamorous room full of people clapping for her. She is glowing, beautiful, adored. Funny. Needed.

But when she stops smiling, the whole room goes silent, everyone’s faces are blank. The audience waits.

She tries to speak honestly, but no sound comes out. They only clap when she smiles.

She stops smiling. The room goes silent. The lights go out one by one.The spotlight becomes harsh. Her smile fades.
 

Samantha’s fear is not loneliness exactly. It is being loved only when she is entertaining. She is tired of performing happiness.

Seedance options:
Samantha in party spotlight. Applause with blank faces. Close-up smile fading. Lights going out.

### Beat 12 — Becca's expanded vision: the argument that never ends

**2:45–3:00**

Becca sees a hallway that repeats forever.

Mina stands at the far end. Every time Becca tries to reach her, a door closes. 

Becca opens one door and finds the same hallway again. Same Mina, same distance.

On the wall: framed photos of the three friends, but in each photo one of them is missing.

Becca’s fear is control. She would rather act like she needs no one than admit someone can hurt her. She avoids vulnerability until the door closes.

Seedance options:
Repeating hallway. Mina at far end. Door closing. Photos with missing friend.

### Beat 13 — Old Mina appears in their visions

**3:00–3:15**

All three girls now see the same thing: Old Mina sitting at their table. She has long dark hair, bangs, and a calm face, serene. She is not frightening. Almost sad. She is older. 

She holds a single lemon in one hand.

She does not threaten them. She waits.

Back in the apartment, one chair at the table is suddenly pulled out.

Samantha stops smiling.

Becca whispers, “Did you see her too?”

Mina says nothing.

Seedance options:
Older Mina at table. Empty chair pulled out. Group staring at chair. Lemon glowing faintly in hand.

## ACT IV — THE CONFESSION RITUAL

**3:15–4:15 — 4 core beats**

This act should slow down emotionally, but not become visually dead. Keep hands, lemons, candles, faces, reflections, glasses, shadows alive. This is where the short film becomes human instead of just magical.

### Beat 14 — Mina understands

**3:15–3:30**

Mina realizes the truth.

Mina says “The lemons aren’t showing the future,”. She continues  “They’re showing what we’re afraid will make the future happen.”

The room becomes very quiet.

Mina cuts a lemon open. The knife sound feels unnaturally loud. Juice runs over the table like gold.

Visual goal: The party is over. The ritual has begun.

Seedance options:
Mina speaking quietly. Knife cutting lemon. Lemon juice across table. Samantha and Becca listening in silence.

### Beat 15 — Blonde confession

**3:30–3:45**

Samantha squeezes lemon juice into a glass. 
Samantha tries to joke first, but can’t. 
Then, Samantha says: “I’m scared that if I stop being fun, no one will stay.”
For once, Samantha does not cover it with a smile.

Mina reaches for her hand.

Becca looks away because it hits too close.

The candles behind Samantha burn brighter for a moment.

Seedance options:
Samantha squeezing lemon. Close-up of her face trying not to cry. Mina taking her hand. Candle flare.

### Beat 16 —  Becca’s confession

**3:45–4:00**

Becca takes the glass.

She says: “I act like I don’t need anyone because needing people feels like giving them a weapon.”

She looks direclty at Mina. This is the first fully honest thing Becca has said all night.

A framed photo on the wall changes: now all three girls are visible together again.

Seedance options:
Becca holding glass. Honest eye contact with Mina. Frame/photo changing subtly. Group of Becca, Mina, and Samantha around table.

### Beat 17 — Mina confession

**4:00–4:15**

Mina finally drinks from the glass.

She says: “I said I was leaving because I wanted to become someone else. But I think I was just afraid you already knew who I was.”

Samantha starts crying quietly.

Becca reaches for Mina’s other hand.

The room goes darker. One by one, the candles blow out. Not violently. Gently. Not like a jump scare. Like the room is exhaling.

At the final candle, complete darkness.

Seedance options:
Mina drinking lemon juice. Mina confession close-up. Candles extinguishing. Full darkness transition.

## ACT V — OLDER MINA / THE CHOICE

**4:15–5:00 — 3 core beats**

This final act must not over-explain. Let it feel mythic but intimate. Older Mina is not a ghost, not a monster, not exactly time travel. She is a possible self. A witness. A consequence.

### Beat 18 — Older Mina arrives

**4:15–4:30**

The lights return, but colder and quieter.

Older Mina sits at the table in the empty chair. She looks like Mina, but older: calmer, wiser, carrying the sadness of many choices and the peace of surviving them.

Older Mina holds the whole lemon, bright, in one hand.

The girls are frozen. She looks mostly at Mina, not the others.

She looks at Mina and says: “Good. You changed it.”

No one asks what she means. That makes it stronger.

Seedance options:
Lights returning. Older Mina revealed at table. Mina staring at her older self. Whole, bright lemon in Older Mina’s hand.

### Beat 19 — The room resets / morning

**4:30–4:45**

Cut to morning.

The apartment is bright and soft.The room looks normal again.

The party is over. Lemons everywhere. 

Samantha sleeps curled on the couch. Becca is asleep on the floor near the table. 

Mina wakes up in a chair, still holding part of a lemon.

Her suitcase is still by the door. 

Her phone alarm rings.

She looks at the suitcase, the ticket. Then at Samantha. Then at Becca.

Visual goal: quiet aftermath, emotional clarity.

Seedance options:
Morning apartment wide shot. Friends asleep among lemons. Mina waking up. Suitcase by door.

### Beat 20 — Final choice

**4:45–5:00**

Mina picks up a lemon. 

She does not leave yet. 

She holds the lemon to one eye and looks directly into camera.

For a second, we see what she sees: not a fixed future, but branching glimpses kinetically montage:

Mina leaving.
Mina staying.
Mina returning.
Mina older.
Mina alone.
Mina laughing with Samantha and Becca.
Mina crying.
Mina free.
All possible.

Not destiny.

A choice.


Then back to Mina’s face.

She smiles — not because everything is solved, but because now she knows it is a choice.

Cut to black.

Final sound: a lemon rolling gently across the table.

Seedance options:
Mina holding lemon to eye. Branching future montage. Mina smile close-up. Lemon rolling on table.

## Character functions

Mina is the emotional center. Her problem is not simply “leaving.” Her problem is that she thinks transformation requires disappearance.

Samantha is the mask of happiness. Her fear is: “If I stop entertaining people, I disappear.”

Becca is the mask of control. Her fear is: “If I need people, they can hurt me.”

Older Mina is not there to give answers. She is there to prove that the future is listening.

## Visual rules for the whole film

Keep the apartment warm and dark: amber candles, green plants, old wood, soft bokeh, blue night outside the windows.

Keep the visions slightly colder, cleaner, emptier, and more symmetrical.

Use lemons as transition devices: lemon covers lens, yellow blur, cut to vision. Lemon juice drip, cut to rain. Candle flame, cut to train light. Reflection in cabinet, cut to future room.

The film should start handheld and social, then become slower and more centered as the ritual takes over. By the end, the camera should feel calm, almost ceremonial.

## Best final structure for DaVinci

Even though each Seedance clip is 15 seconds, don’t lock yourself into 15-second scenes. For the final cut, I’d use many clips like this:

Party clips: 4–8 seconds each.
Vision clips: 2–5 seconds each.
Confession clips: 8–12 seconds each.
Older Mina reveal: let it breathe, maybe 12–15 seconds.
Morning ending: 20–30 seconds total, slower and cleaner.

That will make it feel edited, not assembled.

The trick is to keep the supernatural simple: **the lemons don’t predict the future; they expose the fear that creates it.** That’s the line that holds the whole film together.
