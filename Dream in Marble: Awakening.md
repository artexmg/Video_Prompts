# Dream in Marble: Awakening of the Sea

## Subject / Scene Settings

- Genre: Mythic surrealism, based on the Tako to Ama legend 
- Style: Classical Greek sculpture animation (bas-relief marble reliefs)
- Medium: Animated panel stone relief
- Lighting: Warm museum light, soft top lighting, gentle shadows emphasizing carved depth
- Mood: Serene, dreamlike, rhythmic as a visual poem
- Camera: Animated frontal shot of the marble frame, with each scene animated simultaneusly
- Texture: Aged marble, fine chisel detail, soft glows on highlights, dust motes drifting in light

## Animation Flow (Timing & Loop Logic): each panel animated in sequence, from panel 1 to panel 4 trhough all the sequences.

### Scene 1 – “The Dreamer”
Time : [second 0 to second 3]
The first relief animates, the woman’s chest rises and falls as she sleeps, a faint shimmer of air shows her dream forming.

### Scene 2 -“The Awakening Wave”
Time : [second 3 to second 7]
Scene 2 comes to life, her hair flows like marble silk, the carved ocean waves begin a slow, hypnotic motion.

### Scene 3 – “The Arrival”
Time : [second 6 to second 10]
An octopus sculpture animates, its tentacles undulate gracefully like slow currents; marble spray forms and vanishes in rhythm.

### Scene 4 – “The Union of Dream and Sea”
Time : [second 10 to second 15]
Both the woman and octopus shift subtly, as if breathing in unison, they are in union — a dream becoming real.
They are in perfect harmony, perfect union; waves and breath synchronized, faint marble glow pulsing in rhythm.
Fade out to marble texture, static again — like the dream ending.
