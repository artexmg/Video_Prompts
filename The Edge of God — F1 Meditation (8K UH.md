# The Edge of God — F1 Meditation (8K UHD, 15s hyper- cinematic loop)
## Subject / Scene Settings
- Genre: hyper-real japanimation realism; tone: meditative, spiritual, precise
- Theme: the sacred focus of an F1 driver — man and machine in perfect harmony
- Subject type: single pilot in a crimson F1 car navigating Monaco-like circuit inches from walls
- Style: cinematic realism with painterly anime texture (Ghost in the Shell / Akira tone)
- Lighting: late afternoon sun, gold reflections over asphalt; contrast between light and shadow in curves
- Motion: slow-motion cuts alternating with bursts of speed; focus on micro-movements — tires grazing barriers, reflections sliding over helmet visor
- Color/Grade: metallic reds, deep blacks, golden highlights; film grain; warm desaturated palette
- Lens: 35 mm shallow focus inside cockpit → 85 mm telephoto for corners; deep DOF for straights
- FX: heat haze, vibration micro-shake, dust motes in sunlight, brief tunnel flashes
- Sound Design: immersive; engine hum modulated with heartbeat, tire squeal fading into ambient drone; wind Doppler like a chant
- Music: minimalist piano + sub-bass pulse; tempo syncs to breathing

## Audio
- Voice-over: deep calm male voice, contemplative, cinematic reverb  
  "When you get close enough...  
   when the walls blur and the noise disappears...  
   you don’t think anymore.  
   You don’t drive.  
   You become the road.  
   And for a moment...  
   you get close to God."

- Background: low-engine drone blending into ambient pulse
- Transitions: heartbeat → engine → silence → crescendo → fade to hum

## Scene Sequence
1. **Opening (WS, 2s)** — A still circuit under golden haze; car roars into frame, camera low to asphalt.
2. **Tight Corner (CU, 2s)** — Front tire slides inches from barrier; paint dust flickers off wall.
3. **Cockpit Focus (MS, 3s)** — Pilot’s eyes steady; breath syncs with rev counter; reflection of sky across visor.
4. **Tunnel Burst (WS, 2s)** — Speed inside tunnel; light strobes across surfaces; sound compresses to silence.
5. **Slow-Motion Apex (ECU, 3s)** — Gloved hands micro-adjust steering; droplets of sweat drift; wheel tilt precise.
6. **Final Straight (WS, 3s)** — Camera pulls up; track stretches endlessly; voice echoes
