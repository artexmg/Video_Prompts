# “Stillness of the Jellyfish” (6-Shot DOF Edition)
15 seconds - shallow DOF -  8K  hyper-real underwater cinematography

## Style Cues

“Shallow DOF underwater nature cinematography”
“Foreground clarity, background dream-blur”
“Blue Planet × macro ballet”
Avoid magical or glowing fantasy lighting
Keep everything calm, slow, and realistic

## Format & Look

15s · 30fps · 180° shutter
Hyper-realistic 8K underwater capture
Shallow depth of field throughout — foreground jellyfish sharp, background blurred
Lens halation and soft aquatic bokeh
Color space: DCI-P3, teal–cyan anchors

## SHOT LIST (6 shots · ~2.5s each)

Shot 1 — Soft Descent (0–2.5s)
Lens: 35 mm, shallow DOF
Camera: Slow downward drift
Foreground jellyfish glides beneath the camera, perfectly sharp.
Background jellyfish hover as blurry, diffused silhouettes.
Sunrays slice through water, losing shape in soft blur.
Purpose: Introduce depth; calm descent.

Shot 2 — Side Drift Portrait (2.5–5s)
Lens: 50 mm
Camera: Lateral slide left
One jellyfish in foreground, tentacles drifting past lens.
Background shapes melt into creamy teal blur, emphasizing distance.
Particles create floating bokeh dots.
Purpose: Establish DOF as visual identity.

Shot 3 — Cluster Through Haze (5–7.5s)
Lens: 28 mm
Camera: Push slowly forward through a small group
Foreground jellyfish sharp; midground slightly soft; background heavily blurred.
Layers of jellyfish create depth stacks, like underwater parallax.
Soft caustic flickers bloom into bokeh.
Purpose: Multi-layer DOF depth field.

Shot 4 — Backlit Silhouette (7.5–10s)
Lens: 50 mm, very shallow DOF
Camera: Slow arc around foreground jellyfish
Strong backlight from above; bell edges glow.
Background becomes a luminous blur bowl of cyan gradients.
Occasional gentle focus breath (not a full rack).
Purpose: Mood shift — abstract, dreamy.

Shot 5 — Macro Pulse (10–12.5s)
Lens: 100 mm macro
Camera: Slow upward rise (10–15 cm)
Extreme close-up of bell rim pulsing.
Background disintegrates into soft teal haze and circular bokeh.
Focus is razor-sharp only on a few millimeters of surface.
Purpose: Intimate detail & texture.

Shot 6 — Outward Drift & Fade (12.5–15s)
Lens: 40 mm
Camera: Pull back gently
The main foreground jellyfish drifts away, gradually falling out of focus.
Background remains blurred; a faint silhouette becomes slightly sharper as focus transitions.
Purpose: Close with a soft “goodbye” drift.

## Environment & Lighting
Depth 10–15 m
Soft sunlight filtering from above
Neutral-to-cool color palette
Light particulate matter drifting calmly
Zero turbulence, zero fast motion

## Physics
Jellyfish pulses slow (1 every 3 seconds)
Tentacles flow with high-drag secondary motion
Particles drift opposite camera movement
Caustics ripple gently, blurred at distance

## Sound Design
Subtle underwater hum
Occasional micro bubble trail
Deep, barely audible rumble
No music, no sharp transients
Soft, meditative ambience
