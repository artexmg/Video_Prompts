# Sora2 Prompt — “Neon Pulse” (Music Video, 15 s, 24 fps)

## Concept:
A hyper-realistic 3D K-Pop–style girl, @artexmg.stormysunn,  performing an energetic pop-rock song across a series of cinematic, visually explosive scenes — combining realism with anime energy. She wears a red bomber jacket (slightly unzipped), black tank top, denim shorts with chain detail, green choker, red-tinted glasses, and silver star earrings. No guitar — she performs and interacts with the camera dynamically.

## Scene Flow (≈25 s total)

Scene 1 (0-3 s) – “Intro Glow”
Camera: wide shot, dolly-in from neon city alley reasambling time square in NYC
Visuals: neon signs blink “ROCK”, “GO!”, “WOW!”, light reflections on wet asphalt.
Action: @artexmg.stormysunn steps into frame, adjusts her red jacket, and smiles toward camera.
Mood: bold anticipation, fast heartbeat intro.

Scene 2 (3-6 s) – “Pop Energy”
Camera: orbit at chest height, shallow DoF.
Action: she sings first line passionately, hair moving with fan wind.
FX: slow strobes of pink and blue light flash in sync with beat.

Scene 3 (6-9 s) – “Chorus Kick”
Camera: low-angle shot rising upward.
Action: she jumps slightly, gestures with her hand as glowing words burst behind her (“HEY!”, “YEAH!”, “GO!”).
FX: floating text particles ripple outward; sparks and lens flares accent motion.

Scene 4 (9-12 s) – “Street Rhythm”
Camera: tracking side-shot.
Visuals: walls of graffiti flash animated graphics; puddles reflect her silhouette.
Action: she walks confidently, lipsyncing, eyes glinting under neon reflection.
FX: rhythm lines pulse through reflections like sound waves.

Scene 5 (12-14 s) – “Final Drop”
Camera: tight frontal slow-mo; handheld shake with 3-axis stabilization.
Action: she throws her arm upward as confetti bursts; red jacket flares open.
FX: multicolored confetti, dynamic volumetric lighting from behind.

Scene 6 (14-15 s) – “Logo Bloom / Fade”
Camera: push-in to @artexmg.stormysunn face; she winks, then the scene blows out to white.
FX: overexposed bloom transition; neon signs blink “ROCK”, “GO!”, “WOW!” appears, like in Scene 1 for loopable effect

## Physics & FX Specs

Cloth: satin + cotton simulation with secondary wind reaction (Re≈250).
Hair: soft-body strands; respond to wind turbulence and beat frequency.
Lighting: volumetric neon reflections, high-contrast rim lighting, subtle bloom (radius ≈ 0.02).
Camera lens: 35 mm prime f/1.8 – cinematic depth, smooth bokeh.
Color palette: neon magenta / lime / cyan / amber.
Environment reflections: PBR wet asphalt, specular ≈ 0.45.

## Audio / Cue Sync
Beat: 128 BPM pop-rock, punchy snare with bright synth layer.
0–.4 s : Wind + ambient city hum → beat rise.
0.4–12 s : Vocals kick in, neon signs blink in sync.
12–14 s : Guitar drop / bass kick + confetti.
14–15 s : Echo fade / signature stinger.
