CRITICAL CONSTRAINTS

Preserve 100% of the lion anatomy and proportions from the anchor.
Do not morph, melt, or distort the face, torso or legs.
Paint ribbons behave like thick sculpted acrylic with subtle flexibility, not liquid goo.
The lion never floats:
Ground plane = semi-solid, glossy acrylic surface.
Paws have weight, compress slightly into the paint, cast consistent shadows.
Glossy specular highlights sliding over the paint.

GLOBAL STYLE / LOOK

Hyper-real painterly aesthetic, like a 3D sculpture made of thick oil/acrylic.
Ultra-detailed impasto, visible ridges and swirls.
Palette: turquoise background, pastel magentas, cyans, yellows, whites, and hints of violet.
Soft filmic bloom on bright colors, slightly HDR but natural.
Depth of field used artistically: macro shallow DOF → mid/wide deeper DOF.

PHYSICS & FX

Paint Physics: behaves like a hybrid of liquid + ribbon + soft clay.
Ribbons flex and spring back, not melt.
When kicked or hit, paint bursts into thick droplets and flat smears that quickly settle.
Shockwaves: hoof impacts send out low, circular ripples across the acrylic floor, like a pond made of paint.
Particles: floating paint flakes and petals drift slowly in the air, with light catching their edges.
Lighting: single soft key from upper left, subtle back rim from right, creating glossy highlights on every ridge.
