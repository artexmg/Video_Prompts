[BGM]
Relentless post-apocalyptic hybrid cinematic war score built for sustained mechanized invasion and pursuit, 112–120 BPM with elastic tempo surges under pressure, anchored in low minor tonal gravity and chromatic grind rather than melodic resolution. Driven by massive taiko-style war drums and industrial percussion layers that feel hand-played, physically exhausting, and weight-bearing, reinforced by distorted sub-bass pulses that behave like engine torque and propulsion mass rather than musical notes.

Rhythm is obsessive and mechanical: looping ostinatos that barely evolve but grow heavier through added density — additional drums, metal impacts, clanking chains, hull scrapes, pressure-release hits, and sand-choked industrial collisions. Percussion dominates the mix, forward and aggressive, with minimal reverb — everything feels close, dry, and punishing, as if recorded inside moving war machinery.

Harmonic material is sparse and functional: low string clusters, aggressive brass stabs, and dissonant guitar or processed string attacks used as rhythmic weapons, not melodies. Occasional raw vocal or chant-like elements appear only as distant human grit — breath-driven, shouted, or fragmented — treated as percussive texture rather than lead presence.

Sound design blurs music and environment: engines rev in rhythm, impacts land as downbeats, metallic scrapes replace cymbals, pressure waves and mechanical groans punctuate transitions. Silence is rare and brief. There is no verse or chorus — escalation occurs through density, force, and accumulated mass, not harmonic change.

Overall tone: savage, kinetic, industrial, sun-blasted, survivalist. Music exists to push motion and domination, not to comment emotionally. Feels like war conducted by machines at unstoppable momentum, where rhythm is law and stopping is not an option.
