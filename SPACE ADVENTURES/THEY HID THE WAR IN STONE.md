CORE IDEA (LOCKED)

These are not natural asteroids.
They are ancient siege weapons disguised as debris, hidden for centuries inside the orbital graveyard.
Now they are reactivating.

Each rock lights from within, revealing contained planetary-kill energy under a crust of dead stone.
They do not explode immediately.
They wake, drift, scrape past fortress-cities, crack open, fragment, and begin turning the entire orbit into a collapsing mined belt.

The horror is not impact.
The horror is realization.

CAMERA LAW
FPV / survival-camera / cockpit-adjacent only
Constant forward drift or violent evasive motion
Camera always has a reason to move: collision avoidance, debris dodge, shockwave displacement, structural failure
Strong parallax from foreground wreckage
Heavy depth compression through vertical fortress layers
Roll / yaw / pitch never fully settle
Whip-pans only when threat enters frame
No decorative shake; all instability must come from physical events
Camera should repeatedly slip through impossible near-gaps
PHYSICS LAW
Massive objects move with terrifying inevitability, not fast like bullets
Smaller debris moves fast due to shear, impact, venting, fragmentation
Asteroids glow from internal fracture networks first, then crack, vent, fragment
Fortress structures fail in layers: towers shear, bridges peel, hull plates tumble, internal dust blooms outward
Tiny ships are ants against impossible mass
No magical explosions; every rupture must feel pressure-driven and material-based
Debris fields must remain spatially dense and dangerous
WORLD LOGIC
The orbital corridor is made of abandoned fortress-cities, defense spines, docking masses, and ruin-clusters suspended over the planet
The “asteroids” are embedded across the corridor like dead ballast
Activation spreads in a chain, not all at once
The planet below is alive and vulnerable; its curvature is a constant scale anchor
This is not a fleet battle
This is an old siege system waking back up inside its own ruins
