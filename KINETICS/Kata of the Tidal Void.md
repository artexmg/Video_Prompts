Rapid alternating wide / medium shots
Continuous kata: diagonal cuts, pivots, low sweeps
Ocean folds and recoils with each motion

Camera circles clockwise, never stopping

5–10s | ACCELERATION
Kata speed increases
Samurai spins → overhead strike → reverse cut
Ocean begins orbiting him in layered ribbons
Camera dives inside wave hollows, exits near blade
Taiko rhythm intensifies

10–15s | RESISTANCE
Ocean pushes closer, towering
Threaded crests almost touch the samurai
Kata tightens: short, violent movements
Micro-pauses trigger explosive water surges
Camera shakes subtly under pressure

15–20s | DOMINANCE
Kata reaches peak velocity
Ocean fractures into concentric filament rings
Overhead shot: circular kata path carved into the sea
Crimson undertow pulses beneath ivory foam
Music at maximum intensity

20–23s | FINAL FORM
Samurai executes final decisive kata strike
Camera rushes past blade into negative space
Ocean freezes mid-motion behind him
Threads vibrating, stretched to breaking point
Sound collapses to low hum

23–25s | END BEAT
Samurai immediately transitions, no pose

Final filament snap sound
