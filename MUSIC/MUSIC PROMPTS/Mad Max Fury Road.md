Prompt

Relentless post-apocalyptic hybrid score built for sustained vehicular pursuit, 130–138 BPM with elastic tempo pushes under pressure, centered around low minor tonal gravity with chromatic grind rather than melodic resolution, driven by massive taiko-style war drums and industrial percussion layers that feel hand-played and physically exhausting, reinforced by distorted sub-bass pulses that behave like engine torque rather than notes.

Rhythm is obsessive and mechanical, looping ostinatos that barely evolve but grow heavier through added mass: extra drums, metal hits, clanking chains, brake-screech textures, and sand-choked impacts. Percussion dominates the mix, forward and aggressive, with minimal reverb — everything feels close, dry, and punishing, as if recorded inside moving machinery.

Harmonic content is sparse and functional: low string clusters, aggressive brass stabs, and dissonant guitar or processed string attacks used as rhythmic weapons, not melodies. Occasional short vocal or chant-like elements appear as raw human grit — tribal, shouted, or breath-driven — treated as percussive texture rather than lead performance.

Sound design blurs music and environment: engines rev in rhythm, impacts double as downbeats, metallic scrapes replace cymbals, silence is rare and brief. Arrangement favors constant forward momentum with no traditional verse/chorus structure — the track escalates by density and intensity, not harmonic change.

Overall tone: savage, kinetic, sun-blasted, survivalist. Music exists to push motion, not to comment emotionally. Feels like war conducted at highway speed, where rhythm is the only law and stopping is not an option.
