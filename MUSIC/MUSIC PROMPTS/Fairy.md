Style: cinematic ember storm realism
Mood: restrained, charged, intimate
Movement: environmental-driven micro-motion

RULE UPDATE

❌ No static poses
❌ No character idle freeze
❌ No dramatic gestures

✅ Constant micro-motion (hair, wings, dust)
✅ Wind affects cloth and posture
✅ Camera drifts 3–5% over 25s

AUDIO

STRUCTURE
0–4s

Wide silhouette.

Wind passes across frame.
Wings slightly flex to maintain balance.
Hair shifts.

Camera imperceptibly pushes forward (very slow).

Particles swirl unevenly, not uniformly.

4–8s

One fairy subtly shifts weight from back foot to front.

Not walking — just grounding.

Ash density increases near the ground.

Golden light flickers softly with bass pulse.

8–12s

Head of one fairy turns 10–15 degrees toward the other.

Not dramatic.

Just acknowledgment.

Wind catches wings — small adjustment reflex.

Background ruins shimmer with faint distortion ripple.

12–16s

Storm pressure builds.

Particles move diagonally now, not vertical.

Camera slight lateral drift (1–2 degrees).

Their silhouettes slightly closer in frame.

16–20s

Wing membranes vibrate in stronger gust.

Cloth flutters more visibly.

One fairy slightly steps half a foot forward — not escape — just stabilizing.

Subtle bass intensifies.

20–23s

Short glitch flicker — only background distorts.

They remain solid.

Wind suddenly shifts direction.

Particles swirl around them instead of past them.

23–25s

Wind drops 30%.

Their wings slowly relax.

Camera stops drifting.

Cut to black on unresolved bass note.
