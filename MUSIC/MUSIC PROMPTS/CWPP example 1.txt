[Genre / Era]
Pre-digital recording texture, art-folk / psychedelic/ weightless / suspended environment with early glam undertones.
intimate art-rock phrasing with restrained delivery. Intimate, cinematic, melancholic.

[Tempo & Meter]
~64–68 BPM
4/4, slow pulse, floating feel
Tempo should feel unmoored, emotionally suspended

[Harmony & Tonality]
Centered in C major / A minor ambiguity
Frequent use of IV → V → I resolution delayed or avoided
Suspended chords, added 9ths, gentle modal color
Harmonic movement feels like drifting, not traveling

[Structure (Narrative Form)]
Verse-led storytelling, not chorus-driven
Refrain appears as an emotional checkpoint, not a hook
Ending dissolves rather than resolves

[Instrumentation]
Acoustic guitar (fingerpicked, intimate, close-mic)
Mellotron or analog string pad (soft, slightly unstable)
Sparse piano accents (felt or upright tone)
Minimal bass, warm and rounded
Drums enter late (brushes or soft kit, heartbeat-level)
Subtle analog synth sweeps suggesting a weightless/suspended environment.

[Vocal Direction]
Solo male vocal
Gentle British phrasing, emotionally detached but fragile
Close-mic, audible breath, slight saturation
Delivery feels like reporting emotions, not performing them
Occasional harmony lines that feel distant, like radio chatter

[Production Texture]
analog era warmth
Slight tape wobble
Stereo width used sparingly
Reverb feels cosmic, long tail, but low in mix
Silence and space are intentional

[Emotional Physics]
Awe → isolation → quiet acceptance
No triumph
No panic
Calm loneliness

[Conceptual Core]
A single voice attempting to maintain connection across a degrading, distant system.
Technology fades. Emotion remains.
