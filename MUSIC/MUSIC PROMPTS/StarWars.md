CWPP MUSIC PROMPT — Star-Epic Galactic Overture
Target inspiration (non-literal):
Star Wars main title energy — mythic, ceremonial, heroic, cosmic scale.

CORE MUSICAL CONSTRAINTS (High Weight)
Genre: symphonic sci-fi orchestral score
Tempo: ~108–112 BPM (march-like, forward momentum)
Meter: 4/4 with strong downbeats
Key center: B♭ major → brief modal excursions into Mixolydian & Dorian
Emotional vector: awe → resolve → destiny
Narrative feel: ancient myth told through future technology

HARMONIC & MELODIC LANGUAGE (High Weight)
Broad diatonic heroic theme built on:
perfect fifth leaps
ascending triadic motifs
Melody should feel bold and declarative, not lyrical or jazzy
Brass-led statements answered by string surges
Harmonic rhythm slow and monumental — no busy chord changes
Avoid blues notes, chromatic swagger, or swing feel

INSTRUMENTATION (Very High Weight)
Primary voices:
French horns (hero theme carriers)
Trumpets in unison/octaves for proclamation
Full string section for propulsion and lift

Support:
Low brass (trombones, tuba) reinforcing gravity
Timpani + orchestral bass drum (ceremonial strikes)
Cymbal swells and suspended hits for cosmic scale
Optional color:
Subtle choir “ah / oh” pads (non-lyrical, vowel-only)
Light analog synth pad under orchestra (never leading)
RHYTHMIC & STRUCTURAL SHAPE (Medium–High Weight)
Opening: bold unison statement, no intro fluff
Mid-section: call-and-response between brass and strings
Climax: full orchestra in stacked octaves
Ending: sustained heroic cadence — feels like “the journey begins”
PRODUCTION & TEXTURE (Medium Weight)
Large hall / scoring stage reverb
Wide stereo image, deep front-to-back orchestral depth
Clean, authoritative mix — no distortion, no lo-fi
Timeless, analog-leaning orchestral sound

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS (Important)
❌ No jazz harmony
❌ No pop drum kit
❌ No lyrical verses / modern song form
❌ No direct melodic quotation from known themes
