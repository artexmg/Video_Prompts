Reference Prompt — Yellow Submarine–style generation

(optimized · childlike surrealism · nautical whimsy · communal sing-along)

Prompt:

bright psychedelic sing-along pop with a childlike nautical fantasy tone, mid-tempo march feel around 108–112 BPM, simple diatonic major-key harmony, circular I–IV–V motion that loops with nursery-rhyme inevitability, playful 4/4 stomp-along rhythm designed for group chanting rather than dancing, minimal harmonic tension, everything feels safe, cozy, and absurdly optimistic

production leans deliberately toy-like and theatrical: tubby bass guitar moving in simple root notes, clumsy piano chords struck almost percussively, jangly acoustic strums, cheerful brass and woodwind colors that feel borrowed from a children’s band or a seaside parade, with cartoonish sound effects woven into the arrangement (bubbles, bells, crowd shouts, horn blasts, maritime noises)

vocals are unpolished and communal, sung more than performed—lead voice friendly and conversational, surrounded by a crowd of voices answering in unison, chanting short phrases together, overlapping slightly out of time, as if recorded in a cramped room full of friends rather than a studio

lyrics are ultra-literal and visual, centered on shared living, bright colors, and whimsical undersea imagery: friendly crews, imaginary voyages, simple objects, exaggerated innocence, no metaphor depth, no emotional conflict, just collective joy and playful nonsense

overall aesthetic feels 1960s psychedelic children’s television meets storybook fantasy: bold colors, rounded shapes, naïve optimism, gentle surrealism, and a sense that everyone involved is smiling while making it

the song never “develops” in a traditional sense—it loops, repeats, and invites participation, prioritizing memorability, chantability, and togetherness over musical sophistication

[production note: emphasize warmth, analog softness, and intentional simplicity; nothing sharp, aggressive, or modern]
