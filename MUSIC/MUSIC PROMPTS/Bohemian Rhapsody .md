A theatrical 1970s progressive rock mini-opera built from distinct, contrasting movements rather than verse–chorus repetition.

Movement I (intro): intimate piano-led opening, rubato feel, vulnerable solo male vocal recorded very close to the mic, confessional tone, sparse harmony, dramatic pauses that feel conversational rather than rhythmic.

Movement II: sudden shift into operatic choral layering — stacked male vocals in exaggerated harmony, call-and-response phrases, playful and surreal delivery, rapid dynamic jumps, tongue-in-cheek theatricality, voices treated almost as orchestral sections.

Movement III (climax): explosive hard-rock section with distorted electric guitar, pounding drums, assertive rhythm, aggressive delivery, high emotional release that contrasts sharply with the earlier delicacy.

Final movement: energy resolves with a reflective, resigned tone rather than triumph; tempo relaxes, instrumentation thins, ending feels conclusive but emotionally bittersweet.

Overall feel: dramatic, eccentric, emotionally expansive, genre-blending, unapologetically theatrical.
Analog studio warmth, 1970s rock production sensibility, frequent dynamic and textural contrasts, music unfolds like a short narrative rather than a pop song.
