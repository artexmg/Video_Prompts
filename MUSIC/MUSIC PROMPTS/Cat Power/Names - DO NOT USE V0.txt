Minimalist Emotional Vignette — 25s

CORE INTENT (LOCKED)

This piece is about naming as loss.
Words once meant something. Now they echo.
Emotion is present, but never explained.

No climax.
No resolution.
Just recognition.

TEMPORAL FORMAT (HARD CONSTRAINTS)

Duration: 25.0 seconds

Tempo feel: Free / slow pulse (≈ 56–62 BPM implied, not explicit)
Meter: Undefined / breath-driven
Dynamic range: Narrow, intimate, restrained
Ending: Unresolved decay (no cadence)

EMOTIONAL ARC (ABSTRACTED)
Presence → hesitation → withdrawal → quiet persistence
No “sadness cue.”
Emotion emerges only through space, delay, and restraint.

STRUCTURAL TIMELINE
0.0 – 5.5s | Arrival
Single sustained harmonic element (piano, reed, or filtered pad)
Slight instability: pitch drift, breath noise, mechanical imperfection
Silence is part of the rhythm
Rule: Nothing announces itself.

5.5 – 11.0s | Attempt to Speak
Fragile melodic fragment enters (2–3 notes max)
Human-adjacent articulation: hum, bowed string, voice-like synth
Notes do not resolve; they stop short
Negative space increases
Constraint: No full phrase allowed.

11.0 – 16.5s | Names Without Referents
A second layer mirrors the first, but delayed and degraded
Slight detuning or time smear
Call-and-response that never quite connects
Emotion rises, but volume does not
Rule: Intensity grows internally, not sonically.

16.5 – 22.0s | Letting Go
One layer fades completely
Remaining sound thins to near silence
Rhythm dissolves into breath-length pauses
Visual: subject partially exits frame or is obscured (fog, shadow, motion blur)
Constraint: No replacement for what disappears.

22.0 – 25.0s | After the Name
Final sustained tone, barely audible
Ambient noise (room tone, wind, tape hiss) becomes dominant
Cut ends before emotional closure
Rule: The piece stops, it does not finish.

SONIC LANGUAGE (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
Dry, intimate, close-mic perspective
No reverb tails longer than 1.2s
Imperfection preferred over polish
Human timing > grid timing
Silence treated as content

VISUAL LANGUAGE (IF APPLIED)
Static or near-static composition
No montage, no fast cuts
Natural light only
One subject, one space, no explanation
Movement must feel incidental, not staged

WHAT THIS IS NOT

❌ Not a song
❌ Not a cover
❌ Not nostalgia-as-aesthetic
❌ Not emotional manipulation

This is emotional residue.
