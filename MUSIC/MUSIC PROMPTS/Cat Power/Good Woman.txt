⏱ FORMAT (HARD CONSTRAINTS)

Duration: 25 seconds
Tempo: ~62–68 BPM (slow, breathing pace)
Time Feel: Loose, rubato-friendly
Dynamic Range: Extremely narrow
Loudness: Intimate, almost whisper-level
🎹 INSTRUMENTATION (WEIGHTED)
Primary (Non-Negotiable):
Felt or upright piano (soft hammers, uneven attack)
Solo female vocal, close-mic, dry
Secondary (Barely There):
Low room reverb tail (mono or near-mono)
Occasional pedal noise / key thump
Subtle air hiss (tape or room)

Forbidden:
❌ Percussion
❌ Strings
❌ Pads
❌ Harmony stacks
❌ Any rhythmic insistence

🎤 VOCAL CONSTRAINTS (CRITICAL)
One voice only
Imperfect pitch allowed (even encouraged)
Breath sounds audible
Slight vocal fatigue or fragility
Delivery feels like confession, not performance
Vocal should sound like it was recorded at 2am, alone, after deciding not to argue anymore.

🧠 EMOTIONAL ARC (MICRO-STRUCTURE)

0.0–8.0s
Sparse piano notes, space between phrases
Voice enters hesitantly, almost unfinished thoughts

8.0–16.0s
A single emotional realization
No rise in volume — tension comes from stillness

16.0–23.0s
Voice slightly cracks or thins
Piano simplifies further, maybe just one note repeating

23.0–25.0s
Sound decays naturally
No resolution, no cadence
Just acceptance

🎚 PRODUCTION RULES (LOCKED)
No compression pumping
No stereo widening tricks
No cinematic polish
Keep it human, flawed, vulnerable
Silence is as important as sound

🧩 THEMATIC SUBTEXT (IMPLICIT)
Being “good” didn’t save anything
Love wasn’t loud — it just faded
Strength expressed through restraint
Dignity in letting go

🛑 ABSOLUTE BANS

❌ No crescendo
❌ No big ending
❌ No lyrical clarity spoon-feeding
❌ No dramatic scoring

🧠 FINAL GUIDANCE (META)
This piece should feel like:
You overheard something you weren’t meant to hear — and it changed you.
