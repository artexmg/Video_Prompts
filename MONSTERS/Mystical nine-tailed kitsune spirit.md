Prompt Description

A mystical nine-tailed kitsune spirit rendered in a Japanese brush-art / flowing filament style.
The fox sits in profile, facing left, with a sleek, elongated snout, sharp pointed ears, and a single glowing amber-orange eye radiating supernatural intensity.

Its fur is not realistic hair, but hundreds of fine, ribbon-like strands, resembling paint strokes, silk threads, or liquid filaments, layered and flowing with visible motion.
Primary body fur is deep charcoal black and indigo, transitioning smoothly into teal, cyan, magenta, coral, and crimson tones across the tails.

The nine tails spiral and weave around the body like currents of water or wind, overlapping in elegant arcs, each tail composed of long, textured brush strands.
Loose autumn leaves in red and orange float through the scene, caught in the swirling motion.

Behind the fox, a large full moon dominates the background—textured like thick oil paint or carved clay, glowing warm gold and ivory.
The sky is painterly and abstract, with soft teal, gray, and muted blue strokes, slightly blurred to enhance depth.

Lighting is soft yet dramatic, moonlight rim-lighting the fur strands and tails.
The overall mood is ethereal, calm, mystical, and ancient, with a sense of quiet power.
