The anime depicts the seaside road of Vancouver in summer, inspired by the Stanley Park Seawall. In the distance, we can see the iconic Lions Gate Bridge stretching gracefully across the water, with the downtown Vancouver skyline and forested mountains rising behind it.

The sky is warm and golden, with soft clouds drifting slowly. Gentle waves reflect the city lights and the bridge’s silhouette. Tall evergreen trees line parts of the road, blending nature with the city.

The sea is calm and filled with deep blue waves, while white clouds float peacefully above. The atmosphere is serene, whimsical, and magical.

The style is inspired by Hayao Miyazaki, featuring clean linework, soft shading, rich natural colors, and high-detail textures. Ultra high-definition, high-resolution, and high-quality illustration.
