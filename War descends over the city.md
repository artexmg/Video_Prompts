WAR DESCENDS OVER THE CITY (15s · 30fps · 8K UHD · Hyper-Real Thread-Surrealism · Cinematic)

STYLE & VISUAL LANGUAGE

Hyper-real woven-thread surrealism · destruction-as-textile metaphor · volumetric dust · muted war palette (stone, ash, iron) contrasted with burning-thread reds & yellows · tactile micro-fibers · high dynamic range · slow drifting haze.

MAIN SUBJECT

A colossal eagle forged from endless, tightly-coiled warped strands of colored thread, moving like living fiber muscles.
The eagle = metaphor for war.
The city = metaphor for civilians.

Its body undulates as if made of thousands of independent filaments snapping, tightening, and rippling under tension. Its wings drag across the ground like catastrophic rivers of thread.

ENVIRONMENT

Destroyed stone city · crumbling buildings · dust clouds · fallen debris · broken streets.
Thread-rivers from the eagle seep into the ruins like living veins consuming everything.

FX / PHYSICS
Thread behavior: laminar → turbulent fluid-cloth hybrid simulation
Micro-fibers flicker with subtle light diffusion
Structural destruction: dynamic fracture, dust bloom, granular debris
Thread rivers flow like viscous fabric streams obeying gravity and drag
Atmospheric: drifting ash, suspended particulate haze, soft sunlight beams through dust

SOUND DESIGN
Deep sub-bass rumble of the descending eagle
Fiber tension sounds: elastic snaps, twisting rope creaks
Distant collapsing buildings
Low war-drone tone (ominous, metallic)
Dust whooshes + granular impacts
Final moment: single low heartbeat-like thud

SCENE STRUCTURE (15 seconds)
Scene 0 — Anchor (0.0–0.2s)
Close-up on the eagle’s woven head, hyper-macro of fibers pulling tight, tensioned like sinews preparing to strike. Soft rumble.

Scene 1 — Descent (0.2 - 3.0s)
Wide aerial: the giant thread-eagle descending from the sky, flapping his powerful wings, while dragging kilometers of woven strands. Ruined city revealed below. Dust and debris swirl upward from its downdraft.
Camera: 35mm, slow tilt-down into the chaos.
Physics: fibers ripple with aerodynamic turbulence; buildings shake.

Scene 2 — Impact Glide (3.0–6.5s)

The eagle skims over rooftops, its thread-feathers scraping buildings, ripping stone apart as filament bundles unravel on impact.
Particles: stone fragments, thread shrapnel, drifting ash.
Camera: handheld-style tracking shot at ground level, looking up—gives scale.

Scene 3 — Devouring Sweep (6.5–10.5s)

The eagle dives into the streets.
Its fiber-tendons extend like serpents, wrapping around structures, pulling them apart in waves. Each building collapses in slow, controlled textile-like motion.
Thread colors intensify: reds and yellows flare like burning embers.

Camera: slow orbit around the creature’s head as it tears through the city.

Scene 4 — City Engulfed (10.5–14.0s)

The eagle unfurls its wings, releasing a sweeping tsunami of multicolored threads that consume the city block by block.
Debris turns into dust; the ground becomes a carpet of tangled strands.
Cinematic, tragic, awe-inspiring.

Camera: high aerial shot pulling back to show the scale of devastation.

Scene 5 — End Card (14.0–15.0s)

Fade to black.
White text:
“War descends”
