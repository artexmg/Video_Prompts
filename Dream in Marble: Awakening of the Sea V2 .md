# Dream in Marble: Awakening of the Sea  (15 s - Mythic Surrealism in Bas-Relief Animation)

## Subject / Scene Settings
- **Genre:** Mythic surrealism, inspired by the *Tako to Ama* legend  
- **Style:** Classical Greek bas-relief animation carved from marble  
- **Medium:** Living stone — sculpted forms that move and breathe within a single animated frame  
- **Lighting:** Warm museum top-light; gentle shadows enhancing emphasizing carved depth  
- **Mood:** Serene, dreamlike, meditative rhythmic as a visual poem  — as if the marble itself dreams  
- **Camera:** Slow frontal dolly with micro-parallax and dynamic light shifts across surfaces  
- **Texture:** Aged marble, fine chisel detail, soft glows on highlights, dust motes drifting in light

---

## Animation Flow / Timing (15 s Loop)

- Scene 1 – The Dreamer Breathes (0–3 s) {The woman’s chest rises and falls as if alive. Her hair barely stirs, a halo of dust twirls above her pillow, and a soft pulse of air forms behind her head.}
- Scene 2 – The Awakening Wave (3–6 s) {Marble hair turns fluid as waves ripple through the carved background. Veins of light trace her profile, and subtle echoes of motion radiate from the frame’s edges.}
- Scene 3 – The Arrival of the Sea Spirit (6–9 s) {An octopus emerges in continuous motion from the bas-relief depth. Tentacles curl and unfurl with slow, weighted grace as marble spray particles rise and settle.}
- Scene 4 – The Union of Dream and Sea (9–12 s) {The woman and the octopus intertwine in serene harmony. Their breaths and motions synchronize as pulses of light flow softly across their marble forms; Subtle glowing veins travel through the stone as the two forms move together in tranquil unity.  
}
- Scene 5 – Harmonic Marble (12–15 s) {All elements animate together within one living panel — waves, hair, and tentacles sway in perfect rhythm. A faint radiant pulse travels through the stone before it fades back into still marble grain.}
